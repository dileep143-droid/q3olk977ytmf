Additionally, Fujiko since the cease of ''Time Patrol Bon'' focused more on ''[[Doraemon Long Stories]]'', however he was indeed willing to continue ''Time Patrol Bon'', and wanted to write another episode even before his death.<ref name=Ukita>Nobuyuki Ukita, October 25, 2011, \"About ''Pokonyan'' and ''T.P.Bon'', ''Fujiko F. Fujio Complete Work: Pokonyan'', p.356, Shogakukan, ISBN:978-4091434746</ref>\n\nThe manga consists of three parts, part 1 (14 episodes) followed by part 2 (10 episodes) and part 3 (11 episodes) respectively. Due to the suspension of ''Monthly Shōnen World'' and Fujiko's health issues, part 1 was declined in total episodes, and part 2 and part 3 were irregularly published, and some chapters, up to 15 episodes at one point, were not recorded in comics until 2012 because of his death.\n\nThese<ref group=\"Notes\">Repeated hiatuses and the cancellation of the manga due to the suspension of ''Monthly Shōnen World'' and health issues and the death of Fujiko.</ref>might have also caused the notable inconsistences in the manga, such as the separation of Bon and Ream in the part 1 against the formation of a formal duo by Bon and Yumiko later, [[Yoko Shiraki]]'s eventual replacement with Yumiko, and so on. Various drastic changes made on the 1989 anime were also presumably due to the same reasons. \n\n=== Anime adaptations ===\n[[File:Dorami and Ream and Bon and Yumiko 2016.png|thumb|[[Dorami]] with Ream and Bon and Yumiko in the 2016 film.]]\n[[File:Fujiko-esque mob in Nobitas Treasure Island.png|thumb|A reference to ''Time Patrol Bon'' and ''[[Chinpui]]'' series with [[Tarao Tarako]] in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Treasure Island]]''.]]\nThe 1989 anime adaptation was created by [[wikipedia:Studio Gallop|Studio Gallop]] with the involvement of several staff members from the concurrently running animated adaptation of ''[[wikipedia:Kiteretsu Daihyakka|Kiteretsu Encyclopedia]]'', including [[wikipedia:Kunihiko Yuyama|Kunihiko Yuyama]]. Through the franchise, they have been constantly mingled for differing extents.\n\nIn the manga, Yumiko was originally depicted as a complete stranger to Bon from a different school until Bon engaged in a mission to save her life, and Yoko is instead a close, neighborhood friend of Bon. However as Yumiko became a notable figure for the plot, Yoko was gradually replaced by Yumiko, resulting in Yoko's eventual disappearance from the plot. Yumiko and Yoko had never met directly in the manga.\n\nIn the manga and the 1989 anime, Yumiko replaced both Yoko and [[Ream Stream]] (and [[Buyoyon]]) for differing extents, possibly due to the sudden cancellation of the part 1, repeated hiatuses and irregular releases of part 2 and 3, and the eventual cancellation of the manga itself due to [[Fujiko F. Fujio]]'s health issues and death. Yumiko's first appearance in the franchise was from the part 2 after the retreats of Ream and Buyoyon.\n\nAkin to ''[[wikipedia:Kiteretsu Daihyakka|Kiteretsu Encyclopedia]]'', the 1989 anime was allegedly a trial attempt to produce an animated television series if the special anime has gained sufficient receptions. In this anime adaptation, Yumiko was completely mingled with Yoko, and made her appearance since the very beginning unlike the manga; Yumiko in the 1989 anime has a design reminiscent of Yōko, and Yōko is originally Bon's classmate in the manga instead of Yumiko. In this incarnation, Yumiko calls Bon by name without any honorific title while she in the 2024 anime calls him \"Namihira-san\" akin to Yoko in the manga and the 2024 anime. \n* According to this rumor, the anime special is the prologue which focused to feature [[Ream Stream]], and the potential television series would have featured Yumiko instead.<ref>[https://w.atwiki.jp/aniwotawiki/pages/58949.html 没作品] on Aniwota Wiki</ref>\n\nWhile the 2024 anime is more loyal to the manga to introduce Yoko as a classmate of Bon, Yumiko was instead changed to a student from another class of Bon's school, and Yoko and Yumiko are friends since before. In this adaptation, Yumiko has hair style and coloration that are rather intermediate between Yoko and Yumiko in the manga. This adaptation also depicted Yumiko with more modest personality than in the manga and the 1989 anime, somewhat resembling Yoko's characteristics in the early manga. \n\nYumiko's changes in her design and background in the 2024 anime are presumably to deal with her replacements with Yoko both in the manga and the 1989 anime. These inconsistences, along with others such as the separation of Bon and Ream in the part 1 and the later formation of a formal duo by Bon and Yumiko, were likely caused by the demise of the manga (cancellation of the part 1 and repeated hiatuses and irregular releases of part 2 and 3 chapters, and the eventual cancellation of the manga) due to the suspension of ''Monthly Shōnen World'' and health issues and the death of [[Fujiko F. Fujio]].<ref name=TPBook /><ref name=Ukita>Nobuyuki Ukita, October 25, 2011, \"About ''Pokonyan'' and ''T.P.Bon'', ''Fujiko F.
