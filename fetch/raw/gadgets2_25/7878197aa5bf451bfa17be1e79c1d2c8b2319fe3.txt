As Nobita and Doraemon continue to physically confront one another, Tamako shows up and orders them to stop fighting. After seeing their quarrel, Sewashi starts to question whether sending Doraemon to help Nobita out was a good idea. Dorami tries to convince him otherwise, but Sewashi is still worried and decides to send Dorami to the present to look after Nobita and Doraemon.\n\nBack in the present day, Nobita and Doraemon are still not in good terms as they are eating dinner with their backs turned against each other, much to the dismay of Tamako and Nobisuke. As Doraemon leaves the kitchen and heads back to Nobita's room, Dorami shows up and they have a happy reunion. Dorami tells him that he's been feeling depressed and Doraemon tells her about his troubles with Nobita. Nobita overhears the conversation and angrily calls Doraemon useless before shutting the door and leaving, causing Doraemon to declare that he wants to go back to the future. Later on, Dorami explains what's going on to Sewashi, who asks Dorami to fix the situation.\n\nThe next morning, Dorami tries to wake Nobita up for school and uses the Dream glasses and Dream controller to view Nobita's dream and turn it into a nightmare so that he can wake up. Doraemon asks Dorami whether she wants to play but Dorami states she's going to look after Nobita. Doraemon tries to demotivate her by saying Nobita is hopeless and will never change, but Dorami refuses to lose her faith in him. Dorami takes out her laptop and sees that Nobita has fallen asleep in class once again. Dorami takes out the Dream Controller again and makes it such that Shizuka shows up in Nobita's dream, tells him that she likes him and would like him even more if he stops sleeping and study harder. This motivates Nobita to the point that he wakes up and yells that he'll do his best much to the shock of Sensei and everyone else in class. Doraemon looks on with relief and he and Dorami decide to fly to school to keep a further eye on Nobita. They see Nobita lagging behind at the track and Doraemon comes up with the idea of using a [[Fart Rocket|rocket]] to make Nobita go faster. Dorami advises him not to do that and Doraemon angrilly asks her why. Dorami reveals to him that she came to trade places with him on behalf of Sewashi. Doraemon refuses but Dorami reminds him that he's been worn down by his issues with Nobita. As they continue to see Nobita struggle in track, Dorami takes out the Self Confidence Helmet and Invisible Spray to boost Nobita's confidence and make him run faster on track. This in turn delights Sensei and everyone else in class.\n\nLater that day, Nobita comes back home and is estatic on how good his day has been. He decides to take a nap despite Doraemon's protests and then immediately wakes up thanks to sleeping on Dorami's Instant Napping Pillow. Nobita asks Dorami if she has a gadget that could generate a fun book and Dorami pulls out the Quiz Ray and shoots it on Nobita's backpack. She asks Nobita to take out the books in his backpack and he does only to find two quiz books much to his delight. Nobita works on the quiz books until he's finished and shows them to Dorami who reveals that in reality they're his homework books and that he has finished his homework. This makes Nobita really happy while Doraemon tells him that he got most of his answers wrong. Nobita immediately scoffs him off and Dorami uses the Hustler to help Nobita gain enough energy to go out and play.\n\nAs Doraemon sees Nobita play soccer with his friends, he sadly ponders that Nobita might indeed be better off with Dorami instead of him, recalling how he didn't go to school late, did well on track and even completed his homework thanks to her efforts. As Doraemon arrives back home and into Nobita's room, he finds Sewashi who tells him that he can return back to the future for awhile. Doraemon accepts the offer while hiding the tears that are welling up. Just then, Nobita arrives and greets Sewashi. Sewashi tells him that Dorami is going to take care of him for a few days while Doraemon goes back to the future. Doraemon tells him that he'll become a better robot when he comes back so that they won't fight ever again. Realizing how badly he behaved all this time, Nobita tearfully pleads to Doraemon to not go back to the future, promising that he will not misuse his gadgets ever again and call them useless. Doraemon in turn declares that he doesn't want to go back to the future and the two share a tearful reunion. While all this is happening, Sewashi and Dorami decide to head back to the future, content that the conflict is over.\n\nThe next day, Doraemon and Nobita are quarreling yet again. Once Nobita continues using the gadget outside of practice, the attraction powers begin causing massive problems involving melons, watermelons, Doraemon, and eventually even outer space itself.\n\n== Plot ==\nThe episode begins with Nobita arriving home completely bruised and beaten up, alarming Doraemon. Doraemon immediately assumes Gian and Suneo attacked him again and prepares to confront them, but Nobita explains everything happened during “close-range dodgeball practice” at the vacant lot. In a flashback, Gian claims the intense exercise is necessary training and repeatedly blasts dodgeballs directly into Nobita’s face at point-blank range, leaving Nobita with nosebleeds and bruises while Suneo watches.\n\nWanting to improve Nobita’s dodgeball abilities, Doraemon introduces a gadget called the “Circle Net Belt.” The belt allows the wearer to repel or attract round objects like a magnet. Negative settings repel round objects, while positive settings attract them. Both modes include blue, yellow, and red power levels, with red being the strongest. To demonstrate the gadget, Doraemon places Nobita on a small miniature toy car outside the house. When Doraemon sets the belt to blue negative mode, Nobita instantly dodges the car upon impact. Switching it to blue positive mode causes Doraemon himself to get pulled toward the car and become stuck to it like a magnet, amusing Nobita.\n\nLater at the vacant lot, Nobita uses the Circle Net Belt during dodgeball practice against Gian and Suneo. By activating blue negative mode, Nobita effortlessly dodges every incoming ball. The dodgeballs hilariously ricochet back into Suneo multiple times while Gian exhausts himself throwing endless shots. Eventually both Gian and Suneo become completely tired and leave.\n\nSoon afterward, Nobita encounters Shizuka carrying a bag with two melons, but the melons accidentally roll away. Trying to help, Nobita switches the belt to yellow positive mode, causing the melons to instantly fly toward him. Unfortunately, the belt’s attraction effect prevents him from returning them, making Shizuka mistakenly think Nobita is selfishly stealing both melons for himself before angrily walking away. Things become worse when Nobita passes a watermelon stand while still using yellow positive mode. Multiple watermelons suddenly fly off the vendor’s stall and stick to Nobita’s body. The furious vendor accuses him of theft and demands the melons back. Nobita desperately switches between positive and negative settings, causing the watermelons to repeatedly scatter and reattach themselves around him in complete chaos. At that moment, one watermelon flies upward and accidentally smacks Doraemon in the head. Annoyed and believing Nobita is causing trouble again, Doraemon flies down toward him, only to become magnetically stuck to Nobita due to the belt’s attraction power. Trying to fix the situation, Nobita increases the belt to yellow positive mode, causing both of them to slowly float upward together while Doraemon calmly tells Nobita to handle the gadget more carefully. Every time Doraemon talks, however, Nobita’s hands accidentally tickle him because of how tightly they are stuck together.\n\nDisaster strikes when Nobita accidentally turns the belt all the way to red positive mode, causing both him and Doraemon to slam violently into a giant circular gasoline tank. Panicking, Nobita immediately switches the belt to red negative mode instead, violently separating them in opposite directions. Nobita shoots high into the sky while Doraemon is launched downward into a tree before crashing hard onto the concrete below. Terrified, Nobita begins floating endlessly upward. Doraemon urgently tells him to switch the belt back to maximum positive mode, but Nobita surprisingly points out that doing so would cause him to crash directly into the ground and possibly die. Doraemon briefly becomes impressed by Nobita’s intelligent response before returning to panic mode and warning him that if he keeps floating upward, he could eventually reach the stratosphere where oxygen levels are dangerously low.\n\nRealizing the danger, Doraemon desperately flies upward and tells him to take the belt off immediately. Nobita quickly removes the Circle Net Belt, but doing so causes him to begin falling straight toward the ground at deadly speed. Doraemon manages to save Nobita at the last moment by cushioning his fall, though Doraemon himself ends up buried inside a crater at the vacant lot. Thankfully, he survives the impact. Meanwhile in space, the discarded Circle Net Belt drifts near Earth’s orbit beside an active satellite. Suddenly, the satellite accidentally bumps into the gadget, switching it to maximum red positive mode.\n\nThat night, Nobita asks Doraemon what happened to the belt. Doraemon casually claims the gadget is probably drifting somewhere at the edge of space now. However, Nobita notices the moon strangely appearing much larger and closer than usual. Doraemon dismisses it as imagination, and Nobita reluctantly agrees. In the final scene, the moon silently continues moving closer toward Earth while a distant flashing light in space (likely the activated Circle Net Belt) glimmers ominously in the darkness, ending the episode on a disturbing cliffhanger with their fates unknown.\n\n== Characters ==\n\n* [[Doraemon]]\n* [[Nobita Nobi]]\n* [[Shizuka Minamoto]]\n* [[Suneo Honekawa]]\n* [[Takeshi Gouda]]\n* Unnamed Watermelon Vendor\n\n== Gadgets Used ==\n\n* [[Take-copter]]\n* [[The Circle Net Belt (gadget)]]\n\n== Trivia ==\n\n* This episode is considered one of the darkest endings in the series because the final scene strongly implies the moon may actually be getting pulled closer toward Earth, while Doraemon and Nobita remain completely unaware of the possible disaster.\n* If the moon actually got closer to Earth like the episode heavily implies, it would realistically become a catastrophic disaster.
