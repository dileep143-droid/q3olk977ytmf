At first, the ropes seem like the perfect tool for punishing troublemakers; however, dozens of Snake Justice Ropes escape into town, and they begin tying up everyone from litterers, liars and rule-breakers.\n\n==Plot==\n\nThe episode begins at the vacant lot, where Nobita, Gian, and Suneo are bored. Gian suddenly announces that he wants to hold a concert. To Nobita's horror, Gian demands that he bring at least 100 audience members. Suneo joins in by teasing Nobita and claiming he is responsible for badmouthing Gian. When Nobita fails to find anyone interested, Gian threatens to punish him if he cannot recruit enough people. Back home, Doraemon becomes angry after hearing the story and decides that Gian's behavior deserves justice. He introduces the Fearsome Snake Justice Rope, a basket containing thirty magical ropes shaped like snakes. Whenever someone commits a bad act, the ropes automatically emerge and bind the offender. Doraemon demonstrates the gadget by having Nobita lightly hit him on the head, causing a rope to instantly wrap around Nobita.\n\nTaking the rope for a walk, Nobita and Doraemon witness a cat stealing a fish. Immediately, the rope shoots forward and captures the cat. Soon afterward, they encounter Suneo. When he mocks Nobita's inability to recruit audience members and throws an empty soda can onto the ground, a Snake Justice Rope instantly ties him up for littering. Next, they visit Gian's house. Nobita intentionally tells Gian that he could not find any audience members. Gian immediately attacks him, triggering another Snake Justice Rope. Gian becomes tied up, but things worsen when he kicks Doraemon in frustration, tightening the rope and tying him to a tree. Meanwhile, at home, Tamako enters Nobita's room to put away clean laundry. While tidying up, she accidentally knocks over the basket containing the remaining Snake Justice Ropes. The ropes immediately escape. Mistaking them for real snakes, Tamako screams as dozens of ropes slither out the window and spread across town.\n\nElsewhere, Suneo frees Gian, and the two decide to get revenge on Nobita. Meanwhile, Nobita, Doraemon, and Shizuka spend time together at Shizuka's house. During their conversation, Shizuka's mother asks how violin lessons went. After her mother leaves, Shizuka admits that she skipped practice because she finds the violin difficult and uninteresting. A Snake Justice Rope suddenly bursts through the window and ties her up. Doraemon explains that the ropes not only punish obvious crimes but also react to dishonest behavior. Realizing how dangerous the escaped ropes have become, Doraemon and Nobita rush back toward home in search of the whistle needed to control them.\n\nAlong the way, they witness the ropes causing a mess throughout the neighborhood. One rope ties up a man for throwing a cigarette butt onto the street. Another captures a boy who stole a toy car from a younger child. Soon afterward, a woman is tied up after allowing her dog to leave a dog poop in public, and another rope catches a man who is urinating in public. Although the ropes are technically meant to punish bad behavior, Doraemon begins to realize that someone has knocked over the Justice Snake Rope Basket, and they are enforcing justice far too aggressively and indiscriminately.\n\nLater, Doraemon and Nobita attempt to take a shortcut through a construction area marked with a \"No Entry\" sign. To their embarrassment, the Snake Justice Ropes immediately turn on them as well, tying them up for breaking the rules. At that moment, Gian finally begins his concert with Suneo as his only audience member. As Gian starts singing, Suneo desperately covers his ears. Suddenly, all of the Justice Ropes throughout town begin reacting. Doraemon finally realizes what is happening. The ropes normally respond to a special whistle. Gian's terrible singing sounds almost identical to the whistle's signal. One by one, every Justice Rope abandons its target and returns to the basket. The crisis is finally resolved.\n\nFeeling grateful that Gian unknowingly saved the day, Doraemon suggests they attend the concert as thanks. Neither Nobita nor Doraemon wants to suffer through Gian's singing.
