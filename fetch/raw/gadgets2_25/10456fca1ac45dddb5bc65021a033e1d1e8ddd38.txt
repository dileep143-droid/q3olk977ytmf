After being manufactured in a [[Matsushiba Robot Factory|Factory]] in the 22nd century, he is sent back in time by his owner, [[Sewashi Nobi]], to help his great-great-grandfather, [[Nobita Nobi]], become a successful person and have a better future.\n\nDoraemon was voiced by [[Kōsei Tomita|Kosei Tomita]] in early episodes of the 1973 anime; [[Masako Nozawa]] in later episodes; [[Nobuyo Oyama]] in the 1979 anime; and [[Wasabi Mizuta]] in the 2005 anime.\n\n==Appearance==\nDoraemon is a blue robot cat from the 22nd century. He has white circular palms and feet, a large white face with six whiskers and a red nose, a red collar with a yellow bell attached, a red tail which acts as a power lever, and his [[4D Pocket]] on his belly. Although being made up of metal, he can be squeezed and stretched. As he does not have any ears, there is a running gag where he is mistaken for a Japanese raccoon dog (a seal in the [[Doraemon: Gadget Cat from the Future|US dub]]).<ref>[https://www.jawhm.or.jp/blog/workingholiday-infocenter/2023/03/01/doraemon/ ドラえもんはアザラシ！？突っ込みどころ満載！米国版ドラえもん]</ref> \n\nOriginally, he was yellow and had ears which were eaten by a mouse (a robot mouse that misunderstood an order in the OVA), giving Doraemon his fear of mice. His yellow paint was washed away with his tears, leaving him blue.\n\n<gallery>\nDoraemon2011Movie.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita and the New Steel Troops ~Winged Angels~]]''\nDoraemon2012Movie.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita and the Island of Miracles ~Animal Adventure~]]''\n2013Doraemon.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Secret Gadget Museum]]''\nFile:Artwork Doraemon 2016.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita and the Birth of Japan 2016]]''\nFile:Bnr mypp on dora.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Great Adventure in the Antarctic Kachi Kochi]]''\nFile:Footchara1.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Treasure Island]]''\nFile:Doraemon Character 2019.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Chronicle of the Moon Exploration]]''\nFile:Head chara.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's New Dinosaur]]''\nFile:Doraemon Movie 2021.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Little Star Wars 2021]]''\nFile:Doraemon Movie 2023.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Sky Utopia]]''\nFile:Doraemon Movie 2024.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Earth Symphony]]''\nFile:Doraemon Movie 2025.png|Doraemon in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Art World Tales]]''\nFile:Doraemon Movie 2026.png|Doraemon in [[Doraemon: New Nobita and the Castle of the Undersea Devil]]\nFile:DoSbm.png|Doraemon in ''[[Stand by Me Doraemon]]''\nFile:DoSbm2.png|Doraemon in ''[[Stand by Me Doraemon 2]]''\nFile:DoSbm2withatie.png|Doraemon in [[Stand by Me Doraemon 2]] with a tie on\n</gallery>\n==History==\nDoraemon's history has differed multiple times in various iterations, but several aspects remained consistent.\n\nDoraemon was manufactured in the [[Matsushiba Robot Factory]] in the year 2112 AD, bearing the code MS-903. He was damaged by an electrical bolt during production which detached a critical screw from him, limiting his abilities while crippling a variety of external attachments. He and his peers served as mechanical child caretakers resembling cats, and as he underwent training, his performance was seen as generally subpar, him often being regarded as an outmode. Sometimes before he entered service, he was infatuated with another mechanical cat named [[Noramyako]].\n\nDoraemon was adopted by the parents of an infant [[Sewashi Nobi]] so he could nurture Sewashi, who he quickly bonds with. One day, his ears were nibbled on and damaged by a mouse, giving him a fear of mice. He also gained a fear of doctors when a surgery went haywire and left him bald. He was unable to obtain any replacement ears due to Sewashi's low income. Soon afterwards, Doraemon's yellow bodywork was washed off by his tears, leaving him with just the blue base paint. Later on, his sister model [[Dorami]] is manufactured.\n\nAs per the request of [[Sewashi Nobi|Sewashi]], Doraemon is later dispatched into the past to help Sewashi's great-great grandfather [[Nobita Nobi]] have a good future in an attempt to alter the horrendous history of the Nobi family, setting the franchise's plot into motion. Doraemon, Nobita, and friends embark on many time-traveling adventures using gadgets from his four-dimensional pocket.\n\n==Personality and Characteristics==\nBeing a machine, Doraemon does not have some average needs of a regular person like food. If he does consume food, it is automatically converted to energy within his nuclear furnace. He has sensors that function similarly to organs, and his hide and fuselage are extremely tough, not deforming even when attacked. His sensors can often paralyze him, such as when he encounters biological agents or extreme temperatures. \n\nDoraemon's favorite food is [[Dorayaki]] (known as Yummy Buns in the US dub). It is unclear how he developed this interest, the reason differing between various depicting. Nonetheless, his love of dorayaki often leads him to getting exploited out of one, which leads into a fit of anger like in [[The Mecha Maker/2005 Anime|The Mecha Maker.]] He also likes to eat watermelon, pudding, tea, juice and various kinds of cake. \n\nBeing a robot cat, he is easily infatuated by other cats, living or mechanical. He often acts as a peacekeeper to stop their arguments and gifts many items to them, ignoring his usual hobbies in the process. He has had multiple feline love interests and would pursuing them until he is aware that they are better without him. However, he often gets extremely jealous if his love interests are occupied by others. Having no ears, Doraemon is quite shy around mechanical cats that have any, unless he is already acquainted with them.\n\nDoraemon can get easily annoyed easily if anyone criticizes him for his performance or misidentifies him as a raccoon. His fear of mice and doctors often cause him to overreact, even threatening to detonate a nuclear weapon on the spot to get rid of a rat or refusing to attend to government-mandated robot check-ups. If in a panic, Doraemon may mess up his current task carelessly and produce hordes of irrelevant items from his 4D pocket looking for the gadget in need, much to the annoyance of others.\n\n==Name origin==\nThe name \"Doraemon\" can roughly be translated to \"stray.\" The Japanese name ドラえもん is written in a mixture of two Japanese scripts: Katakana (ドラ) and Hiragana (えもん). The \"Dora\" is not derived from ''dora'', meaning gong, but instead from \"dora neko\" (brazen or stray cat), and is a corruption of ''nora'' (stray). The \"emon\" is a component of male names (e.g. Goemon) that was popular in the 20th century. The franchise plays on the homophony of \"Dora\", with Doraemon loving grilled Dorayaki.\n\n==Relationships==\n===[[Nobita Nobi]]===\n[[File:Doraemon_&_Nobita.png|thumb|240px|Doraemon hugs Nobita after his return from the future in [[Goodbye, Doraemon]]]]\nNobita is Doraemon's very best friend. As seen in many episodes, they have a strong brotherly bond and would never leave each other's side, despite their many quarrels and disagreements. \n\nWhenever Nobita has a problem or is in trouble, such as needing to finish homework, being bullied by Gian and Suneo, or wanting to impress Shizuka, he often runs off to Doraemon begging for him help or to make him feel better. Doraemon almost always succumbs to his needs to in the end, usually pulling out one of his many gadgets for the current situation. Occasionally, Doraemon sometimes rejects assisting Nobita, usually if he is fed up with the latter's constant whining and codependence on him. Nobita sometimes calls Doraemon \"useless\" because of this, which may make Doraemon angry and want to prove Nobita wrong by using his gadgets to help him out. The idea was approved. Dorami first appeared in the March 1973 issue, but was depicted as Doraemon's \"girlfriend\" rather than his sister. Also, her design was different. Dorami debuted formally in April 1973, now as Doraemon's sister. The girlfriend role would be given to another character [[Noramyako]] instead.\n\nThe \"mi\" part of the name is most likely based on the common naming for females in Japan.<!--*Her name seems to come from the French term Ami (friend), from Latin Amīcus.-->\n\n==Appearance==\n[[File:A and B types.jpeg|thumb|left|The female type, formally as the \"B\" type, is a potential prototype for Dorami, [[Noramyako]], and other female robots of the same model (while this does not apply to other versions such as both of the 1980 and the 2005 animes).]]\n\nDorami is a cobalt yellow robot with a red bow (instead of ears) and a flower tail instead of usual ball-shaped tail. Her body is similar to most other models of her type though 4D pocket has red crossed stripes on it and her eyes are \"similar\" to [[Shizuka Minamoto]]. To fit her purpose, the scientist did not install ears and replaced them with a red bow.\n\nThey are considered siblings since they were injected with fuel from the same canister. As mentioned by the scientist's frog assistant, Dorami was lubricated with sterilized fuel drawn from the dense parts located near the bottom, in contrast her brother who was fed with the contaminated top slick.\n\nBeing an advanced model, Dorami has superior intelligence. In general she is a responsible, kind and sensible robot, allowing her to appeal most others, but can still be enraged and provoked by disruptive behavior (such as Dora the Kid attacking people at random). She is in many ways the opposite of Doraemon, but they both are eager to help others when they are in trouble. She produces more horse power as a machine compared to Doraemon despite her size. \n\nIn general, she likes '''[[Melon bread]]''' and other kind individuals, has a dislike for general untidiness, and is terrified of cockroaches. She also hates the \"Pufferfish\" alias.\n\nDespite being an advanced model compared to Doraemon, Dorami is not watertight, whereas her brother is.\n\nHer gadgets are usually depicted to appear as more \"girly\", like her pink take-copter and flowery Anywhere Door. Once they leave the sheet, everything returns to normal, and they simply head home for snacks. \n\nNobita convinces Shizuka to try it. Although she initially thinks they are too old to play such games, she eventually agrees. Once inside the sheet, the two begin acting like a married couple. Shizuka asks Nobita to call her simply \"Shizuka\" rather than \"Shizuka-chan,\" causing both of them to become extremely embarrassed. Nobita promises to buy it for her someday. Shizuka openly admits that she likes him, causing Nobita to blush even harder. Watching the entire scene, Doraemon becomes so overwhelmed by the romantic atmosphere that he simply leaves and heads home.\n\nUnfortunately, the situation changes when Chako returns and sees Nobita and Shizuka together. Since she previously used the Future Playhouse Sheet with Nobita nearby, she innocently calls out, \"You're my husband, Nobita!\" Shizuka immediately misunderstands the situation. Believing Nobita somehow has another wife, she becomes furious. An argument breaks out, and the two begin fighting when Shizuka scratches Nobita like a cat, leaving him covered in scratches. He runs away crying. When Shizuka chases after him, she accidentally steps outside the sheet. The gadget's effects instantly disappear, leaving her confused about why she had become so angry in the first place. \n\nBack at home, Doraemon is shocked to find Nobita covered in scratches despite how well everything seemed to be going earlier. That night during dinner, Tamako notices the scratches on Nobita’s face and becomes concerned. Because of what happened earlier, Nobita suddenly declares that he has completely lost faith in marriage, shocking Tamako with the unexpected response. Doraemon quickly covers for him by saying Nobita is just talking in his sleep, then gently tells Nobita not to think too deeply about it, since it was just a small incident inside a gadget.\n\nThe phone rings. Nobita answers and discovers that Suneo is calling. Suneo reveals that he secretly borrowed the Future Playhouse Sheet from the vacant lot and allowed his parents to use it. To his surprise, the gadget helped them reconcile. Looking through the living room window, Suneo watches his parents happily feeding each other and acting affectionate again. Although Suneo is slightly embarrassed by how lovey-dovey they have become, he laughs and admits that, as long as they are getting along again, everything is fine.
