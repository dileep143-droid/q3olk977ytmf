heat after losing a baseball game. Gian and Suneo blame the defeat not only on Nobita’s clumsiness but also on the unbearable temperature. Wanting to cool off, the two decide to head to the swimming pool, leaving Nobita alone on the street. Feeling insulted, Nobita angrily tells them he would never spend time with people like them even if he knew how to swim, before making a face at them and storming off. As he walks down another street, he suddenly spots Shizuka together with Dekisugi, instantly filling him with jealousy. After Shizuka says goodbye to Dekisugi, Nobita quickly runs up to her and reminds her about his birthday. However, Shizuka struggles to remember the details, which deeply upsets him. Nobita asks if he can spend time with her, but she politely refuses because she has important things to do, leaving Nobita feeling even more miserable.\n\nBack at home, Nobita tearfully complains to Doraemon that Shizuka has become cold and mean because she forgot about his birthday and spent time with Dekisugi instead. While crying, Nobita notices Doraemon using a strange new gadget and tries asking him about it. Doraemon tells him not to interrupt because he is busy searching for Mii-chan. After locating the area he was looking for, Doraemon quickly flies out of the house. Curious, Nobita uses a pair of special glasses that allow him to observe distant events. Through the glasses, he sees a man angrily scolding Mii-chan for fishing in his pond while holding her by the scruff of her neck. Moments later, Doraemon rushes in heroically and rescues Mii-chan, making Nobita proud of him.\n\nHowever, Nobita’s attention quickly shifts back toward Shizuka. He decides to secretly observe her through the gadget, but Doraemon suddenly smacks him on the back of the head and scolds him, explaining that the Sealed Space Probe is not meant for spying on people. Soon after, they notice Dekisugi arriving at Shizuka’s house. Nobita becomes anxious and dramatically tells Doraemon that if Shizuka marries Dekisugi in the future, then Nobisuke and Sewashi might never exist. His worries grow even worse when he sees Dekisugi helping Shizuka with her homework. Nobita shamelessly copies the answers for himself, which frustrates Doraemon enough to leave the room.\n\nAfter Nobita finishes copying the homework, he notices Shizuka and Dekisugi leaving the room. Curious again, he switches the camera feed and discovers the two of them cooking together in the kitchen instead. Nobita immediately becomes hysterical with jealousy and begins pounding on the roof of Shizuka's house in frustration. The violent shaking accidentally causes a small earthquake, making ingredients fall off the table at Shizuka’s house. Frightened, Shizuka instinctively clings to Dekisugi. After Nobita calms down, he briefly feels satisfied because the ingredients fell as this cause Shizuka to start crying, but Nobita's happiness quickly disappears when Dekisugi comforts her. Dekisugi suggests continuing to make the cake despite the accident, and although Shizuka worries that there are not enough ingredients left, Dekisugi gently explains that the size does not matter because food made with sincerity is what truly counts, so the two continue baking together. Even though Doraemon reassures Nobita that Shizuka and Dekisugi would never forget his birthday, Nobita completely refuses to believe him and had a massive emotional meltdown, crying loudly that his birthday has already been ruined.\n\nFinally, Shizuka and Dekisugi arrive at Nobita’s house carrying a present. The moment Shizuka hands him the gift, Nobita becomes overwhelmed with shame for misunderstanding everything and runs upstairs in tears.
