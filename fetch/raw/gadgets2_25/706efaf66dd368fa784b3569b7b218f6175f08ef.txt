Nobita is angry and goes home to ask Doraemon if the wish will really come true? Doraemon takes out the \"Wish Tanabata Rocket\" and thinks it should be...? But Doraemon feels something is wrong. He takes the test stick first, and Nobita writes \"I want pocket money\" because he has 10 yen left. After writing, he hangs it on the rocket. Doraemon says that if he goes out and circles around, it should come true. As a result, Nobita does not see any money after going around, but the remaining 10 yen is lost. Doraemon finds out that it is the \"Anti-Tanabata Rocket\", a prank prop that will make wishes come true upside down. After Nobita and Doraemon quarreled, Doraemon was so angry that he left in a time machine, and Nobita was so angry that he left the room. Nobita later thought of a clever idea. He went back to the room and used a test stick to write \"I want to lose my pocket money\". After hanging it on the rocket, his mother told Nobita that she had 100 yuan in her pocket when she washed his pants. After Nobita understood how to use the \"Anti-Tanabata Rocket\", there are seven wishing sticks and seven kinds of wishes. As soon as Nobita goes out, his mother calls him to ask if he has finished his homework. Nobita makes his first wish, \"I want to become stupid and get zero points in every exam.\" When he goes out, he sees Shizuka and Dekisugi together again, and makes his second wish in anger, \"My relationship with Shizuka has become very bad.\" Seeing Suneo and Gian playing with remote-controlled toy in an empty  lot, he makes his third wish, \"I don't want pocket money, I hope I can never afford remote-controlled toys\" and his fourth wish, \"I want to be beaten by Gian every day.\" Later, Suneo and Gian asks Nobita what he is mumbling about. After Nobita tells them about the gadget, Gian and Suneo ask Nobita to take them to make a wish, but Nobita is unhappy. Doraemon goes to complain to the department store, and before Nobita goes home, he replaces the \"Anti-Tanabata Rocket\" with the normal \"Wish Tanabata Rocket.\" At Nobita's house, Gian and Suneo make wishes such as \"I want to be disliked by many girls (Suneo)\" and \"I want to be the worst singer in the world (Gian)\" (excluding Nobita's wish). After using up six wishing sticks, Nobita is anxious to say that he wants to write one too. Suneo helps him write one saying \"I want to be unhappy\" but deliberately writes \"I want to be happy (Nobita)\". Nobita wants to stop them but they stop him and say that everyone makes mistakes. After the rocket takes off, Doraemon appears and Nobita cries to Doraemon, but Doraemon says that it has been changed to \"Tanabata Wish Rocket\". Nobita is very happy after hearing this (because his wish will really come true), while Gian and Suneo are shocked!
