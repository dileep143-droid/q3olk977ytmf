versions, is one of the main characters of the ''Doraemon'' franchise. \n\nHe sometimes serves as the secondary antagonist in some comic chapters and animated episodes when he is bad or evil of the franchise.\n\nHe is a spoiled rich kid who likes to show off his cool stuff to his friends and make them jealous. The friends he hangs out with mostly are [[Nobita Nobi|Nobita]], [[Doraemon]], [[Takeshi Gouda|Gian]] and [[Shizuka Minamoto|Shizuka]]. He sometimes brags to his friends to things like meeting up with famous stars.\n\nHe gets around ¥5000 (US$40, £25.65) in monthly pocket money and buys cool toys and comic books, but he never lets Nobita touch his comics and cool toys, which provokes his anger and jealousy. He usually hangs out with Gian to think he is the coolest kid in the world like Gian and often bullies, irritates and/or beats up Nobita with Gian. Sometimes, his encomiastic tendencies come into play against Gian's threats. He is also a member of the Giants baseball team and while he isn't particularly proficient, he still somewhat surpasses the weaker Nobita.\n\nHe is portrayed as a rather weak sidekick, only offering help to Gian for personal gain, although sometimes Gian demands from or even threatens him.\n\n== Appearance==\nHe is a short and very thin boy with black hair and big black eyes. \n\n<tabber>1973 anime=\n[[File:Suneo_1973.jpg|thumb|left|Suneo in the 1973 anime]]\nHe usually wears a teal/seafoam-green shirt, brown pants, red socks, and black shoes. His highest and most \"easy-to-see\" personality is narcissism, in which often he likes to look in the mirror and talk about how handsome he is (like the queen in Snow White). Some of the stories start with Suneo showing off some new video game or toy which evokes Nobita's envy and irritation. He has extensive knowledge of science and is a talented artist and designer.\n\nHe has the habit of inviting Gian and Shizuka to see something or go somewhere but always leaves Nobita out with one excuse or another. When he did invite Nobita to go somewhere, it's either to provoke Nobita or under threat from Shizuka. He is also cunning and has a way with words, that even Gian is always pleased by his \"praise\" in singing and taking adults' hearts by praising them. However, despite his arrogant personality, he has a deep side which is a caring, kind, sensitive, supportive, and regretful side. It is shown in many episodes that deep down, he actually feels sorry for Nobita when Gian is beating him up.\n\nHe is still a bed-wetter so he sleeps in the bathroom. He considers this humiliating habit as a major weakness in his composure. Suneo is also very self-conscious about his height, being the shortest kid in his class. His personality when he is in trouble or his friend's life is in danger however, is quite different and he is a lot braver and is ready to help his friends. He's the only one in the group who acts insane during a situation, showing more of his psychotic/insane side.\n\nSuneo is proven to be very careless of the environment when he tried to dump garbage into the ocean much to Doraemon's rage and passion for nature. He is also quite cruel towards animals. For instance, he purposely teased one of the macaques that resemble Nobita by offering it a banana but then swipes it away from the monkey. He even once attempted to catch a rabbit that Nobita first found along with Gian.\n\nSuneo's high intelligence of tactics and strategies is best shown in [[Peeking-a-boo Hat/2005 Anime|Peeking-a-boo Hat]], where he took on the shrewd revenge on Gian for telling the owner of the manga to [[Sensei]]. With the aid of Doraemon's gadget, he executed the plan, sucessfully tricked Sensei and retrieved his manga back. Not only that, Suneo also blackmailed Gian's 0 point test paper to Sensei where he hid in in another manga, granting his successful revenge on Gian.\n\nWhenever he is in so much pain or have been greatly humiliated, he usually cries for his mother.\n\n==Family==\n[[Suneo's mother]] gives him things without considering whether or not it's good for him.\n\n[[Suneo's father]] isn't described in great detail, but it is mentioned that he has friends who are comic writers, movie directors, etc., and that he's a very successful businessman in his life, which contributed to the family wealth.\n\nHe has a younger brother, [[Sunetsugu Honekawa|Sunetsugu]], who lives in New York with [[Suneo's uncle]]. He respects him a lot, but only because of his lies and bragging.\n\n[[Suneo's grandmother]] uses old wives' stories to impart values and good habits to Suneo.\n\nHe also has a cousin called [[Sunekichi Honekawa|Sunekichi]] who seems to be similarly wealthy.\n\n==Relationships==\n\n===[[Doraemon]]===\n{{Quote|Doraemon! Whenever Suneo flaunts his material wealth with everyone, Nobita's jealousy is very easily provoked. He is often seen with Gian being forced to bully Nobita, though deep down he sometimes actually feels sorry for him. He has a habit of inviting Gian and Shizuka to something or someplace, but almost always excludes Nobita with a weak excuse. (A common excuse being \"There is only place for three people\" or he directly insults Nobita to make him very miserable). He is shown to be quite sadistic when it comes to ruining Nobita's good mood with or without Gian around. He seems to enjoy destroying Nobita's self-esteem and smile. Suneo sometimes blames Nobita for every bad deed Gian comes to know about, like drawing Gian on the school board. Despite their height difference, Suneo considers himself tougher and way better than Nobita in every way, which makes him quite intimidating to Nobita even without Gian around. Even Suneo would betray Nobita by pinning his misdemeanors on him in order to avoid possible punishment and Gian's violent confrontations. Nobita almost always gets back at him for said events with one of Doraemon's gadgets. He'd even steal them from him either because Nobita's tendency of showing them off to him or simply he wanted them for his own selfish purposes. On the other side of this, they are shown to be laughing and joking together in some scenes, and they sometimes gang up on Gian (possibly as revenge for Suneo's forced affiliation with Gian, and Nobita's constant beatings). Nobita and Suneo are seen as good friends when they are not arguing and conflicting with each other.\n\n===[[Shizuka Minamoto]]===\n{{Quote|Suneo-san, you're an amazing artist!|Shizuka commenting on Suneo's drawing.|color = Pink}}\n[[File:Doraemonsong13.png|thumb|left|250px|Suneo and Shizuka]]\nSuneo and Shizuka are good friends. While on a hunting trip with his father, he befriended with a feral wolf cub he named Roo.\n\nDuring the start of the film, Kukuru went fishing at the river not far from his village, however at the same time his village was attacked by the [[Kurayami Tribe]] and everyone in his village was taken. Kukuru returned only to find that his village has been reduced to ashes, he was then sucked in by a mysterious vortex which transported him to Japan 70,000 years in the future. Kukuru unknowingly took residence on the roof of [[Nobita Nobi|Nobita]]'s home and secretly stole the Nobi family's food in order to survive. Kukuru experiences a nightmare that showed his village was attacked and that he was unable to save his mother from the Dark Tribe.\n\n[[File:Kukuru sleep.png|thumb|left|Kukuru resting (2016 movie).]]\nThe next day, while exploring Nobita's room Kukuru had an unexpected encounter with [[Takeshi Goda|Jaian]] and [[Suneo Honekawa|Suneo]]. He reacts with hostility and attacks them both. However, he was defeated by Jaian. Doraemon and the others took him back to their settlement in prehistoric Japan to treat his wounds and to figure out his origins. Kukuru soon awakens and was kindly greeted by Doraemon and the others. Kukuru tells Doraemon and the others of his origins, as well as the barbaric Dark Tribe and their fearsome leader Gigazombie who have been terrorizing his people. Doraemon and the others offer their assistance to rescue the Light Tribe, but Kukuru declines their offer and sets out to rescue his tribe. Doraemon was able to convince Kukuru that he is a powerful shaman (with the help of his futuristic gadgets) and offers his assistance in defeating Gigazombie. Doraemon and the others eventually reached north and encounter the Dark Tribe and the living claydol Tsuchidama. Doraemon and the others were able to fend off the Dark Tribe and destroy Tsuchidama, Kukuru was reunited with his parents and the Hikari Tribe.\n\nAfter being transferred to prehistoric Japan, the Hikari Tribe begins to build their new settlement. At first, Nobita enjoys being treated more kindly at school, but things quickly become awkward when Gian unexpectedly falls in love with Nobiko. Will Nobita be able to fix the misunderstanding before Gian discovers the truth?\n\n==Plot==\nThe episode begins at the vacant lot, where Nobita once again loses to Takeshi Goda (Gian) and complains to Doraemon that he wishes he could become a girl. In response, Doraemon introduces a gadget called the “Transformation Headband.” To demonstrate how it works, Doraemon puts on the headband and transforms into a female version of himself named “Dorako.” However, Nobita reacts with disgust, immediately angering Doraemon enough to remove the gadget. Wanting to try it himself, Nobita quickly puts on the Transformation Headband and transforms into a girl named “Nobiko.” Doraemon explains that while the headband is active, everyone around Nobita will naturally recognize him as a real girl instead of a boy.\n\nSoon after, Tamako enters the room to bring snacks, causing Nobiko to panic at first, thinking she would get scolded for dressing like a girl. Instead, Tamako casually calls her “Nobiko,” proving the gadget’s effects are working perfectly. Doraemon then reminds Nobita to change his voice to sound more feminine so nobody becomes suspicious. During snack time, Tamako even scolds Nobiko for sitting carelessly while wearing a skirt, forcing Nobita to sit properly like Shizuka. Later, Nobita heads to school while secretly keeping the Transformation Headband despite Doraemon warning him to return it afterward.\n\nAt school, Gian and Suneo prepare a prank for Nobita by placing a chalkboard eraser above the classroom door. Before entering, Nobita secretly transforms back into Nobiko and walks inside, causing the eraser prank to accidentally hit “her” instead. The boys immediately stop laughing after realizing the victim is a girl instead of Nobita. Shizuka and the other girls quickly defend Nobiko and scold Gian and Suneo for bullying her. As Nobita experiences how differently girls are treated, Gian unexpectedly begins developing feelings for Nobiko after seeing her without glasses. From that moment onward, Gian constantly helps Nobiko throughout the school day, including carrying heavy objects for her, lending school materials, and even protecting her from being scolded by Sensei.\n\nAfter school, Shizuka invites Nobiko over for a girls-only gathering, but Nobita quickly becomes bored by the calm conversations and activities. Meanwhile, Doraemon receives a letter from Gian addressed to Nobiko, asking her to meet him at the riverbank. Fearing that Gian may have discovered the truth and wants revenge, Doraemon and Nobiko nervously head to the meeting spot. However, instead of attacking her, Gian awkwardly asks what Nobiko thinks about him. After Nobiko compliments him for being kind and manly, Gian suddenly leans closer and finally confesses that he has fallen in love with her. Doraemon immediately panics as the situation spirals out of control. Terrified by Gian’s confession, Nobiko steps backward while Gian is trying to kiss her, causing the Transformation Headband to fall off and instantly turning back into Nobita. Realizing he had unknowingly fallen in love with Nobita the entire time, Gian becomes furious and chases him in circles while Suneo stands nearby in complete shock.
