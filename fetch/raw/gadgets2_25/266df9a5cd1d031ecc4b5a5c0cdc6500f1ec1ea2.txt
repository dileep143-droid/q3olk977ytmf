Allows the user to travel to any point in the past or future. It serves as the entrance to the '''[[Time Tunnel]]''' and lets the user visit different times and places by setting the desired date and location.\n\n==Appearance==\nThe Time Machine consists of a computer operator and a panel consisting of few handles and switches. It has minimum one seat for the driver to sit. It also consists of an antennae like projection(s) which collects signals.\n\n[[Doraemon]]'s Time Machine is the most commonly seen and used time machine in the franchise. It consists of a nearly-square-shaped chassis, with various appliances mounting on it, such as controls, antennae, etc. The machine is magnetically powered. It originally has the capacity to hold up to three people at maximum. But since it had broken down once, Doraemon upgraded the gadget, and it is now able to transport up to five people. It is placed in the upper drawer of [[Nobita Nobi|Nobita]]'s desk in Nobita's room in Nobi's Residence. In the [[Doraemon (1973 anime)|1973 anime]], Doraemon's Time Machine had two seats like a car's interior and two levers. It was fully purple coloured. The antennae didn't exist.\n\n[[Pawaemon]]'s Time Machine can be seen in [[Battle! Cat-Model Robots vs. Dog-Model Robots]]. The overall appearance of his Time Machine remains the same as Doraemon's except it has different colors.\n\n[[Dorami]]'s Time Machine is more advanced as compared to Doraemon's. It is more spacious and comfortable from inside. It is an orange (sometimes red) tulip-shaped machine with its leaf-like base. Furthermore, it has three antennae like projections at the top which seems to appear like the pistil of a flower. Dorami's time machine is placed in the mirror of her room.\n\nThe thief's Time Machine which appeared as [[A Visitor from the Country of the Future]] was black coloured, having a pair of yellow and red lights front and back.\n\n[[Time Patrol]]'s Time Machines resembles like a police car having black and white colour with a small red tail like bulb which glows at the top. They are shaped like a computer mouse. The word \"Police\" is written on it's on both sides.\n\nIn [[Doraemon: Nobita's Dinosaur]], [[Black Mask]]'s Time Machine was flat and had his face on it. It had an antenna and a chair. His time hole was rectangular shaped. In [[Doraemon: Nobita's Dinosaur 2006]], his Time Machine was highly advanced and was more comfortable with fewer controls. It was big and wide and was designed like a king's seat. It basically resembles a pink door that acts as a convenient portal.\n\n==History==\nThe history of the Anywhere Door is seen in the 2013 movie, ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Secret Gadget Museum]]''.\n\nThe first ever prototype of the Anywhere Door is gigantic, almost as tall as a three-story house, created by Dr. Hartman. The design was unlike its newer successors, with the door sliding out from the sides of the door. In the movie, Nobita and the others went through it instantly like the current version of Dokodemo Door, but it is highly possible that it has been programmed to warp visitors instantly to the Robots Halls.\n\nThe other newer prototypes are significantly smaller, about the size of a mansion's door. Some of them have no doors, which looks like a warp gate instead. Some of the prototypes have a computer next to it, allowing its users to key in their destinations. Most of the designs are quite contemporary- if not just typically modern.\n\n==Function==\n[[File:Thedoraemonsrobotschool anywheredoorwarning.jpg|thumb|300px|[[Principal Teraodai]] explaining how to use Anywhere Door.]]\nThe Anywhere Door opens a portal to the user's demand, regardless if said or not, with several limitations:\n* Can only cover up to 100,000 light years between two ends.\n* Cannot access other dimensions or parallel worlds. (in episode [[The Horizon in Nobita's Room]])\n* Doesn't function under strong electromagnetic forces.\n* The range of destinations can vary, and is heavily affected by the time period it is used in. Periods farther in the past far from modern civilization, such as prehistoric period, do not have recorded map, and the user has to record it themselves while traveling in that era. \n* Once the user teleports to the other side and closes the door, the departing end's portal will vanish, thus the user should never leave the door  open under any circumstances, or they could risk being unable to return if someone closes the door from the other side.\n* Like a normal door, it is possible to lock it and prevent other people from following the user.\n* Though the destination is chosen through the user's voice, it can also be chosen by attaching a photo of an item or creature to the door. The door then decides the destination through the location of said item or creature.\n* In ''[[Doraemon: Nobita and the Kingdom of Clouds|Nobita and the Kingdom of Clouds]]'', It is possible to use the Anywhere Door as a limited form of Time Machine by tampering with the door knob, which can change the time of the user's destination.\n\n==Appearances==\n<tabber>\n|-|Manga Chapters=\n;Original Manga\n*[[Chapter 542:Shark in the Lot]]\n;Doraemon Special\n*[[Chapter 32: The Seventh Mystery]]\n;Plus Chapters\n*[[Chapter 10: A Preference Photo Printer]]\n;Color Works\n*\n|-|Episodes=\n;1973 Anime\n*\n;1979 Anime\n*[[A Preference Photo Printer/1979 Anime]]\n*[[Romance in the Snowy Mountain]]\n;2005 Anime\n*[[I'm Mini Doraemon]]\n*[[Magical Girl Shizu-chan/2005 Anime]]\n|-|Movies=\n;1979 Anime\n*[[Doraemon: Nobita and the Haunts of Evil]]\n*[[Doraemon: Nobita and the Kingdom of Clouds]]\n*[[Doraemon: Nobita and the Spiral City]]\n;2005 Anime\n*[[Doraemon: Nobita's Secret Gadget Museum]]\n*[[Doraemon: Nobita in the New Haunts of Evil ~Peko and the Exploration Party of 5~]]\n|-|Video Games=\n*[[Anywhere Door Hide-and-Seek]]\n*[[Doraemon: Yume Dorobou to 7 Nin no Gozansu]]\n*[[Doraemon no Game Boy de Asobouyo: Deluxe 10]]\n*[[Doraemon Kimi to Pet no Monogatari]]\n*[[Doraemon: Nobita and the Green Giant Legend DS]]\n*[[Doraemon Dorayaki Shop Story]]\n</tabber>\n\n==Appearance in Games==\n===''Nobita and the Green Giant Legend DS''===\nThe Anywhere Door is one of the gadgets which cannot be used directly in the game. The partner character (either Doraemon and/or Nobita) and the Assist Character (Gian, Suneo and Shizuka) will stay on the Secret Base which the lower screen is for. Fortunately, Sensei hears the commotion and defends him, scolding the girls for believing a rumor without any evidence. Around the same time at the Empty Lot, Gian and Suneo laugh at their successful rumor-spreading, which they initiated as revenge against Nobita for telling Mrs. Goda about Gian's bullying and Suneo's narcissism. (Though, almost everyone in the Doraemon universe is unaware of Doraemon.)\n\nBack in Nobita's room, Doraemon is extremely furious about the situation where Nobita is being falsely accused and that he enjoys carrots for snacks. Once the flower turns into seeds, he blows them toward Tamako, who is ironing a shirt downstairs in the living room.\n\nThe gadget soon takes effect, causing Tamako to rush upstairs and give Doraemon a huge plate of Dorayaki as a reward for \"helping to clean the park area.\" After she leaves, Doraemon presses the button on the gadget to remove the rumor. He then lends the gadget to Nobita along with a potion to use in case the flower doesn’t sprout. However, he warns Nobita not to use more than one drop of the potion, or a big problem could arise. Nobita agrees and leaves the house. \n\nNobita begins his revenge by first heading to the roadway at the back of the mountain. He uses the gadget to spread the rumor that Gian has a tail, which quickly circulates throughout the town, although Gian is unaware of it until he interrogates Suneo. The next day at school, during the teachers' morning speech, Gian jumps onto the stage and pulls down his pants to show everyone that he doesn't have a tail. Unfortunately, the microphone falls and drags down his underwear, exposing his buttocks and causing the girls to scream, creating quite a morning chaos. Satisfied with the result, Nobita heads to the school roof to initiate the second stage of his plan. He spreads the rumor that Suneo has a crush on a fat girl in their class, which greatly torments Suneo both psychologically and physically. However, with Shizuka still believing the false rumor and unable to trust him, Nobita decides to use the gadget again to fully dispel all the false rumors. They manage to avoid a swarm of crows and head back to Nobita's room. There, they attempt to press the button to remove the rumor but are unable to due to its overwhelming growth. The only option now is to wait for 75 days for the rumor to disappear on its own.
