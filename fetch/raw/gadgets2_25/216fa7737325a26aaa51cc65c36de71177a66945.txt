Can Doraemon and the others recover the lost gadget before Nobita disappears forever?\n\n== Plot ==\nThe episode begins at school where Nobita once again receives a zero on his test. Seeing the result, Suneo jokingly tells Nobita that if he keeps failing like this, he may eventually be forced to return all the way back to first grade. Completely horrified, Nobita immediately runs to Sensei for reassurance, only for Sensei to jokingly agree that if Nobita continues getting zeroes, it really could happen someday. Panicking from the thought of being surrounded by first graders again, Nobita runs all the way home in fear.\n\nBack at home, Nobita complains to Doraemon that the joke went too far, and even Doraemon admits the teasing was rather cruel. To cheer him up, Doraemon introduces a gadget called the “Joking Microphone,” which temporarily turns any spoken joke into reality until the user says the phrase “Just kidding,\" reverting back to normal. To demonstrate the gadget, Doraemon uses it on Tamako, who is struggling to fit into a skirt. Doraemon jokes that she becomes so light she starts floating from happiness, causing Tamako to suddenly float into the air in surprise. His head is made of rubber, and his body is a plastic bucket. His hands are two rubber balls; despite not being able to play rock-paper-scissors with them, some comic panels and animated scenes do depict him with mitten-like fingers (similar to [[Doraemon]]). He is approximately 50 centimeters tall and 30 centimeters wide.\n\nKorosuke’s body colors have changed significantly between the publication of the original manga and the release of the 1987 TV special. In earlier color comic media, his head was pink or orange, and his body was red or yellow.\n\nIn the 1987 special, his head color was changed to light yellow, his body to yellow, and his nose to blue. Additionally, he now carried a samurai sword on his back, which had a yellow tip and a black sheath.\n\nFrom the anime series onward, his palette was changed yet again. His light yellow head color was kept, but his body is orange, his nose and two circles are now red, and his sword’s sheath is red.\n\n==Personality==\nKorosuke is a robot that can speak, behave and emote like normal human beings. His behaviors and mannerisms are close to that of a young child. In the Japanese version, he is a naive and extremely stupid character, but he has a heart of gold and is willing to help anyone.\n\nHe is loyal to Kiteretsu and helps him in his inventions, though he will be vocal if he feels an invention is dangerous.
