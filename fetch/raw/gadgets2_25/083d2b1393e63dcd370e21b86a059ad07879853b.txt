He didn't possess cat ears. Instead; El Matadora grew ox horns on his head, which usually poses an unintentional threat to people around him (despite his protests).\n\nHe lives in 17th-century Spain; where he disguises himself by working as a dishwasher at a Gyūdon restaurant. Under the name of love, peace and justice, he transforms himself into his heroic alter-ego: the Keikai-Dora.\n\nEl Matadora earned his name from Matador and Doraemon's name. This is not the result of gender swapping.\n\nEl Matadora is one of the other doras (other than [[Doraemon]], sometimes [[Dora Med III]], and [[Dora-rinho]]) who uses the [[Fourth-dimensional Pocket|fourth-dimensional pocket]].\n\n==Personality==\nFlirty, macho, brash, and coarse &mdash; the best words to describe the personality of Matadora. However, whenever he sees a beautiful girl, somehow his violent attitude vanishes into the thin air.\n\nHe becomes polite and expresses greetings; complimenting them as señorita, while clamping a rose in his mouth. As seen in Seven Mysteries Of The Robot School, he calls Dorami '''Señorita'''.\n\n==Favorite way to eat [[Dorayaki]]==\nEl Matadora's way of eating dorayaki is with spaghetti sauce on it.\n\n==Skills and abilities==\nMatadora possesses the greatest strength amongst all of the Doraemons. It's depicted in the manga that he was able to swing around a bull without effort. Ironically, this poses a problem in his daily life, for he is constantly breaking dishes.\n\nIn the Robolympics (Olympic Games for robots), he participates in weightlifting and lifted an adult elephant all by himself. Though it was an impressive feat, he was disqualified because requirements were to lift the barbells.\n\nHis dream is to be the best matador out there. Thus; he has mastery of his Magic Cloak, where he swings it to blow away his enemy or deflecting bullets.\n\n==Weakness==\n[[File:Thedoraemonsrobotschool matadorabankvault1.jpg|left|thumb|Matadora, wanting to try sleeping in money.]]\nHe loves napping and able to do it at any place; which usually comes at the weirdest time.\n\n==Personal life==\n===Normal===\nMatadora lives with the quick-tempered owner chef, and his daughter [[Carmen]]. He can change back when he calms down or when he realizes he's stepping in water.\n\nDora-med's variant of Doraemon's fourth-dimensional Pocket is the four-dimensional genie lamp. Nobita goes through it once, then goes outside and finds that the stairs that normally leads to the first floor are now going up. However, after going up, he ends up in the kitchen. After reaching the entrance hall, he worries that if he goes out of the house, he will find a first-floor window and fall out. But when he does go out of the house, he is back in the same spot. Finding it amusing, Nobita wanders around for a long time. Doraemon warns him that if he keeps doing this, it will turn into a giant maze from which he can never get out. Nobita's mother realizes it is going to rain and hurries back to collect the blankets, but when she opens the door, she finds herself in his room. Nobita and Doraemon discover that the maze has become very complicated, and even his shortcut gadgets doesn't work, which makes her angry because she has found the gadget. Nobita thinks he can escape through the window, but even after leaving the window, he is still inside. Just as darkness falls and the three are exhausted, their father comes home from get off work. He walks through the front door but goes into Nobita's room instead. Just as he is wondering what is going on, he sees the \"Amazing Maze\" and turns it randomly.
