It consists of a pale pink and dark pink leotard with white rectangular accents and she still dons her necklace,arm bracelet and boots\n\nWhen Shizuka found Riruru, she was dressed in bandages at first, then she had a green blouse,a short white pinafore with two pink buttons, and pink flats. she also accessories with a teal star necklace.\n\nafter being reborn she wears a plain white singlet dress with the same star necklace.\n\nIn the original 1986 movie Riruru's hair was a darker pink and her eyes were a blue-green hue she first wore a dark blue singlet with purple stripes and a pale pink skirt with pink flats when she first met nobita later on she wears a yellow collared blouse and a yellow skirt with white flats.\n\n==Personality==\nDuring the beginning, she was loyal to the Mechatopian army. She used her innocent appearance to trick Nobita into giving the Mirror Pool and built the frontline base for the attack. After Nobita and his friends discovered her actual intentions, she revealed her identity and went directly against them. She was stubborn and would disobey their words of peace. However, her views about humanity drastically changed when Shizuka treated the wounds she inherited from the blast of the mirror world portal.\n\nFurther, Pippo too, tried to change her views about humanity. She was violently enquired by the Mechatopia army leaders in the mirror world, during which she expressed her now changed views. Her words are suppressed by the leader and she is \"disposed\" by the army. She then begins her acts against Mechatopia. She agrees with Shizuka on going back in time and changing the programs the ancient scientist made. After he collapses, Riruru selflessly completes reprogramming the structure, which makes her disappear. Riruru thanks Nobita and asks him two favors, to allow her to borrow the Mirror Pool for a while, and to keep their meeting a secret. Angered, the General hits her, electrocutes her and then orders Riruru to be imprisoned (executed in 2011 remake) as a traitor. In the remake, a firing squad was about to kill her, she asked one last time to stop the invasion. The General gets angry and orders to kill her right then and there. Gouda|Gian]]. Her first appearance was in the first chapter of the manga.\n\n==Appearance==\nJaiko is a short and chubby girl with short black hair almost physically similar to her brother [[Takeshi Gouda|Gian]]. She usually wears a red overall skirt and white top underneath and a beret in the 1979 anime with a manga artist's hat. In the 2005 anime, she has a green top with a flower on it and a manga artist's hat.\n\n==Personality==\nAt the start of the story, Jaiko was a snobby and mean person who liked to play pranks on [[Nobita Nobi|Nobita]]. This personality of her however, is short lived and in her later appearances she is shown as a kind and considerate person. Jaiko's nice personality is the total opposite of Gian's hot-blooded nature. In the 2005 remake, Jaiko's personality is the same as her later appearance. She is also shown to be skilled in cooking and sewing. Her original personality is presumably used to create [[Botako]], an ugly and nasty girl that rarely made an appearance.\n\nAs as aspiring mangaka, Jaiko goes by her amateur Mangaka mangakastyle pen name '''Christine Goda''' (クリスチーネ 剛田 ''Kurisuchīne Gōda''), and sometimes submits her manga to publishing companies for prizes. She mostly fails in these contest because her storytelling is still rough. When she decides to give up writing comic books, Gian's friends are asked or forced to cheer for her. Jaiko formerly had a crush on a guy named Motesugi. She has a crush on [[Moteo Mote|Moteo]], a seventh grader in the school. She is sometimes referred to as Hana.\n\nShe loves to write comic books and draw. In the future, she will be a famous writer with lots of copies of her comic books sold.\n\n== Family ==\n\n*'''[[Takeshi Gouda]]'' - '''''Jaiko and Takeshi are siblings and although most siblings fight, both of them get along well with each other, compared to her brother's cruel behavior towards his friends, with Nobita being the target of his bullying.\n*'''[[Mrs. Gouda]]''' - Mrs. Goda is the mother of Jaiko Gouda. Instead of being upset, the two celebrate, believing this is the perfect excuse to convince Tamako to buy a large new television. The cloth can manipulate time depending on which side is used. One side moves time forward while the other moves it backward. Doraemon demonstrates by covering the old television with the cloth, instantly restoring it to a brand-new condition. Excited by the result, Nobita uses the cloth on the washing machine. Watching from outside the house, Suneo becomes interested in the cloth and immediately recruits Gian to help him steal it. Meanwhile, Nobita and Doraemon discover a painter searching through a junk area for furniture to decorate his house before a friend arrives from the countryside. Secretly, they use the Flying Time Cloth to restore discarded furniture into beautiful pieces, greatly surprising the grateful painter. Not long afterward, Gian and Suneo put their plan into motion. While pretending to play soccer nearby, Gian deliberately bumps into Nobita and Doraemon. During the confusion, they successfully swapped the real Flying Time Cloth for a fake one they had prepared beforehand. Neither Doraemon nor Nobita notices the swap.\n\nUnfortunately for the thieves, things go wrong immediately. Gian is sent home by his mother to help wrap two boxes of eggs. While experimenting with the Flying Time Cloth, he accidentally uses the wrong side. The eggs advance rapidly in time and hatch into chicks. As Gian desperately chases the escaping chicks, the Flying Time Cloth is blown away by the wind. At Dekisugi's house, Dekisugi asks them to repair his computer, and the gadget mysteriously fails to work. Realizing they have been tricked, they begin searching for Gian and Suneo. Suneo eventually finds the cloth and returns baby Gian to Jaiko before taking the gadget home.\n\nDoraemon and Nobita finally track the cloth to Suneo's house and ask for it back. A giant fox, crocodile, and snake are now loose inside Suneo's room and have cornered both Suneo and his mother on top of his study desk. She energizes him with a [[Speedwinder]] and he takes Dorami out to play. He makes another gadget for Shizuka to grow flowers all-year round, and receives some requests from other classmates to make new gadgets. Still, Nobita feels despondent.\n\nDorami returns to the future the next day and finds Doraemon feeling unwell, too, and believes that Nobita should be taken care of Doraemon.
