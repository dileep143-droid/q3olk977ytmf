Thus, the diary is one of the most powerful yet dangerous gadgets of Doraemon.\n\n== Appearance ==\nThe Future Diary resembles a small diary consisting of a bright and vibrant cover with geometric patterns. In the middle of a diamond-shaped yellow border is a red, rectangular gem. The spine alternates between green and red segments.\n\nHowever, in [[Doraemon: Nobita's Earth Symphony|''Doraemon: Nobita's Earth Symphony'']], the Future Diary is much larger, comparable to the size of an average textbook. The cover of the diary is quite different, appearing mostly brown and tan, but it is more elegant and subdued. In the center is a diamond-shaped red gem bordered with gold, inset into an X-shaped pattern. Each corner of the X-shaped pattern has tiny gold square embellishments. The differences imply Doraemon has countless variations of the book.\n<gallery hideaddbutton=\"true\" bordercolor=\"transparent\" captionalign=\"center\" position=\"center\" spacing=\"small\">\nFile:Future Diary II.png|The Future Diary as seen in ''[[Doraemon: Nobita's Earth Symphony]]''\nFile:Future Diary I.png|When the diary is opened.\n</gallery>\n\n== Features ==\nThe diary realizes whatever the user writes on it, i.e., the content becomes true or real. It is impossible to counteract the diary because once written, it is precisely as it is. Whatever might neutralize the contents written is nullified by the diary, including, but not limited to, items, innate abilities, technology, et cetera. This diary primarily alters reality or probability. This diary apparently bypasses immunity.\n\nThe user can tear the page off to 'cancel' the effect. The user can also write something that overrides the previous content (e.g., writing \"Nobita cannot sleep\" to counteract \"Nobita can sleep\"). Additionally, the user can either use the [[Time Cloth]] to revert the page to when it was blank or burn it entirely. The erasers do not work on the content whatsoever.\n\nBesides reality warping, the diary can summon any food the user desires. The diary can control the target's actions and emotions, assigning tasks for the target until they complete them, as well as inducing love, mind-controlling, and manipulating technology. In addition, the diary can erase a concept from existence and negate anything related to it.
