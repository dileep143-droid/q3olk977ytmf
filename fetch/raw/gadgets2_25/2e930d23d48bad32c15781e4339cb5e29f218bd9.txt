As he stops, a bank employee reminds him parking is prohibited. Denja pretends to apologize, then grabs his expanding portable artillery and begins nuking the bank, creating several incendiary blasts. As he walks in the burning rubble, he continues firing his gun as the camera zooms into his eye.\n\nIn the modern day, Nobita pinches and squeezes Doraemon's elastic nose, remarking he might be able to substitute it with a red marble. Doraemon suddenly wakes up and becomes curious, though Nobita denies even asking the question, and gives a similar negative response when Doraemon inquires on the clay pile he is working on, which he considers oversized. While Nobita walks off to find additional materials, Doraemon decides to enjoy his Premium Dorayaki, which he claims queuing three hours for, though not before rubbing it around his face. Nobita ends up losing his balance when retrieving a box and collapses just as Doraemon attempts to devour it, causing the latter to embark on a frantic search. When Nobita gets to his senses, he turns his head around, and Doraemon screams in anguish at the macabre sight of the squashed Dorayaki.\n\nMeanwhile, Dorami is on her way to Nobita's home on a time machine, still complaining of Doraemon not attending his annual check-ups. Just as she pops up from the drawer portal, a pillow smacks her in the face. Nobita attempts to excuse himself while an enraged Doraemon pursues him around, continually highlighting the value of his destroyed snack, as the pillow drops, with both fighting each other off. Nobita claims it would have ended with an apology, though Doraemon cites if that ever resolves everything his presence is meaningless. They insult each other, and engage in a violent brawl until Dorami's shout forces them to yield. In a fit of rage, Doraemon declares his intentions to return permanently and hops into the drawer portal, finding Nobita unbearable. They refuse to bulge, taunting each other with facial expressions as Doraemon sinks back in. Nobita sighs, and Dorami comforts him, claiming she would pacify and convince Doraemon. Nobita suddenly turns around, begging for her promises to deliver, while also mentioning his attempt to celebrate Doraemon's birthday, as she nods in agreement. The camera pans back at the clay mound on Nobita's desk.\n\nDenja brute forces his way out of the bank with three bags of cash in his grasp. Tiny security bots attempting to stall him were pummeled by his devastating melee strikes, ending with his throw destroying a group of them. Finding the authorities closing onto him, he makes a sarcastic comment on actually pursing him despite their duties and hops into his vehicle. Groups of police hovercraft give chase, though he easily wards them off with a pulse mine, knocking his enemies airborne in a massive, green plume. Gloating on his victory, Denja suddenly encounters a dog on the freeway and swerves, only for his vehicle to skid, impact and rotate multiple times until it violently explodes in a green flash. As the terrified puppy flees, the light quickly subsides, and black smoke rises from the wreckage.\n\nDorami attempts to explain Nobita wasn't actually evil, though he rebukes with Nobita's tendencies mostly unknown to her. She then attempts reverse psychology, declaring he might have understood Nobita better. He was quick to realize his logical fallacy, but eventually concedes to Dorami's request of reconciliation. Unfortunately, he had completely forgotten Dorami was taking him to medical checkups, much to his terror, as she corners him. As he is being pushed, Doraemon claims to have a cramp and is continually annoyed. Eventually, he dupes Dorami with the Substitution Rope as she chastises him, only for her to panic upon assuming control of his body. The officers draw their energy pistols on him, though Doraemon ends up also destroying the handcuffs unintentionally. After school, he encounters Doraemon, who introduces him the Lightning Rod, a gadget designed to store and utilize lightning. As Nobita excitedly experiments with the Lightning Rod, he inadvertently causes chaos, hurting himself and his friends during his reckless use of the gadget. With escalating problems and a dangerous malfunction, will Nobita confront the consequences of his actions?\n\n==Plot==\n\nThe episode begins at school, where the Sensei demonstrates static electricity to his students by rubbing a sheet and places it on top of his head. Suddenly, a lightning strike occurs, which frightens Nobita. However, the rest of the students, including Suneo and Gian, are excited despite the Sensei's instructions to be quiet and return to their seats. When a second lightning strike happens, Nobita hides behind a locker and confesses to Suneo and Gian that he is afraid of lightning. This prompts the entire classroom to laugh at Nobita.\n\nAfter school, Nobita spotted Doraemon on the rooftop and headed to his room. Once inside, Nobita asked Doraemon about the gadget he was holding. Doraemon explained that it was called the Lightning Box, which stores lightning inside the box. The lightning that occurs earlier, Doraemon uses it to store inside. He then brought out another gadget called the Lightning Rod, which requires a lightning charge. Doraemon placed the Lightning Rod on the small thunder section of the Lightning Box to charge it. Next, Doraemon demonstrated how the Lightning Rod works by helping Tamako jump-start the microwave. However, as soon as Nobita took hold of the Lightning Rod, Nobisuke asked him to cool down the water in the bathtub. Nobita, about to wave his arm, accidentally shocked Nobisuke.  Back in the room, Doraemon scolded Nobita, explaining the dangers of the Lightning Rod, such as how a voltage of 10,000 watts can cause electrocution when combined with water and electricity. Just then, a mouse appeared beside Doraemon, and when Nobita pointed it out, Doraemon panicked. In his panic, multiple shockwaves were released. Nobita then asked Doraemon to give him the Lightning Rod, which Doraemon reluctantly handed over. Nobita used it to shock the mouse away. Since Nobita saved Doraemon once again, he wanted to borrow the gadget a little longer. Although Doraemon agreed, he reminded Nobita to be more careful with it.\n\nOutside, Nobita was engaged in a variety of activities while using his gadget. First, he jumpstarted a broken radio, which caused both the cat and dog to screech in pain. Then he helped a man retrieve his ring that had fallen under his car. Later, he saw Shizuka and her friend playing tennis when a ball was about to hit him. He used the gadget to slow it down, but despite his efforts, the ball struck the gadget, leaving a small crack on the lightning rod. Next, he assisted Suneo by retrieving his new toy plane from a tree, but it broke and crashed to the ground just moments later, causing Suneo to become angry. As Nobita noticed the crack on his gadget, lightning bolts surrounded him, and Suneo, worried about the situation, told Nobita to stay away. However, Nobita's mischief activated the gadget, which seemed to act as revenge for Suneo's humiliation of him at school. The scene then shifted to Doraemon in the air, who was searching for Nobita. He heard Suneo screaming and rushed to see what was happening. Suneo explained that Nobita had electrocuted him by hugging him and pointed Doraemon in the direction Nobita was heading. Doraemon quickly set off to find him.\n\nIn the next scene, Gian and his pet Muku are out for a walk when Nobita approaches them.
