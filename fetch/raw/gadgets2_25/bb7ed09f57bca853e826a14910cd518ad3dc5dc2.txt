However, because fighting has already become a habit for him, Gian asked Doraemon to borrow a gadget called, “Back At You Gloves” to help control Gian’s temper. Kaori happily thanks Gian for helping her dog, and Gian instantly falls in love with her at first sight. Overjoyed and full of confidence, Gian loudly sings while heading home, but his mood is ruined after Nobita accidentally bumps into him. Because Gian is too distracted thinking about Kaori and cannot even remember what she looks like properly, he angrily takes his frustration out on Nobita and beats him up. Nobita runs home crying to Doraemon, who decides to help by bringing out a gadget called the “Back At You Gloves.” The gloves cause any attack made by the wearer to rebound back onto themselves. However, Doraemon struggles to think of a way to make Gian wear them.\n\nThe next day, Gian accidentally breaks Suneo’s remote-controlled car. Nobita and Suneo are left completely shocked after hearing Gian suddenly say something so unbelievable.\n\nWanting to test Gian’s promise, Suneo later provokes him on purpose. However, Gian instinctively punches Suneo without thinking, causing Gian to finally realize that hitting people has become such a natural habit for him that he cannot control it anymore. Feeling guilty, Gian borrows the Back At You Gloves from Doraemon and begins wearing them all the time to prevent himself from hurting others. Because of this, Gian slowly stops hitting people altogether, making everyone around him extremely happy thanks to Hanano Kaori.\n\nThe next morning, Suneo Honekawa suddenly runs toward Nobita and Doraemon in a panic, revealing that Hanano Kaori has returned to America.
