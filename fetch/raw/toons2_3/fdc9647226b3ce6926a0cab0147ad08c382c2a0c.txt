Mitsuo experiences numerous close calls with his secret identity being revealed among the five known Permans. \n\n== Appearance ==\nMitsuo is an elementary school boy with black hair with bangs parting to both sides, and a horn-like spike. He usually wears a white shirt with green pants. As Perman, he wears a blue helmet, a red cape, and a red badge shaped like the letter “P.” His copy-robot is either yellow or white. \n\n== Personality ==\nHe is an average fifth-grader who is motivated to help others in need. He is depicted as an average, relatable 11-year-old boy. He has a crush on his classmate [[Michiko Sawada]], and often quarrels with [[Sumire Hoshino|Perko]]. He is a big fan of the idol [[Sumire Hoshino]]. \n\nMitsuo doesn't have any stand-out qualities, doing poorly in both academics and sports. As such, he's looked down upon by most of his classmates. However, he is a kind and selfless person, with a strong sense of justice; often putting others above himself. He's a people-pleaser by nature, which often puts him into trouble. Despite this, he has incredible willpower, and can score well in both academics and sports if he puts in the effort. \n\nHowever, behind this clumsy exterior lies Perman No. 1, the leader of the Earth's Permans. As Perman, his behavior becomes more heroic, as he now has the strength to back up his sense of justice. His superhero identity is incredibly popular. Balancing homework, a secret identity, and saving the day often leaves Mitsuo in incredibly tight spots. \n\nPerman No. 1 and Mitsuo are seen as best friends by people who don't know his true identity. As a result, people often visit Mitsuo when they want to ask Perman for help. \n\n== Relationships ==\n=== Family ===\n==== [[Mrs. Suwa]] ====\nMrs. Suwa cares for her son, but sometimes scolds Mitsuo for getting low scores on tests, not cleaning his room & stealing food from the refrigerator. In one episode, Mitsuo gets a slightly better score on his test, but she still got disappointed, making Mitsuo angry. Later, she realizes that Mitsuo had put in actual effort on the test. \n\n==== [[Ganko Suwa]] ====\nAs siblings they fight with each other. Mitsuo dislikes her, because she tattles about him to her mom. When Ganko learns that Mitsuo has a crush on Michiko, she blackmails him with the information so later, he cleans her room for her in exchange of keeping that information a secret. His mom always thinks Ganko does all the chores, so she praises Ganko rather than Mitsuo who actually cleaned her room. However, Mitsuo does care about his sister and looks out for her even when he is Perman. Similarly, Ganko is shown to have sisterly affection towards her brother as when Kabao tries to beat him, and being very worried when Mitsuo gets kidnapped. \n\n=== Friends ===\n==== [[Booby]] ====\nMitsuo's relationship with Booby is that of close teammates and friends. Both are chosen by Birdman to become members of the Perman team, and they work together to fight crime and help people. Mitsuo, as Perman No. 1, is more thoughtful but often clumsy, while Booby, as Perman No. 2, is a chimpanzee with a kind heart but limited intelligence. Aside from Perko, Booby is Mitsuo's closest friend as Perman. \n\n==== [[Sumire Hoshino]] ====\n''For more information on their relationship and the list of scenes that was centered around them, see [[Mitsuo Suwa and Sumire Hoshino|Relationship Between Mitsuo Suwa and Sumire Hoshino]]'' \n[[File:Screenshot 2026-03-26 231916.png|thumb|Perman Perko Hugging in Goodbye Perko]]\nMitsuo greatly admires Sumire on TV. He even dreams once to marry her (as shown in the episode, \"The balloon with lucky card\"). Unknown to him Perman #3 is Sumire Hoshino with whom he often quarrels. He views her as a tomboy, who can never become a good girl. Perko often tries to show her love as in one episode, she tried to take the letter which was meant for Michiko. Perko's never afraid to speak her mind, whereas Mitsuo has no idea about his emotions for her for a long time. It can be seen in many episodes, he horrifies at the talk of any romantic relation with Perko. However, as the series progresses, he slowly starts accepting these things and coming in terms with his feelings. Infact, there have been multiple hints in the series itself showing Mitsuo's growing affection towards Perko. Although they butt heads often, Mitsuo cares about Perko and doesn't hesitate in saving her from dangerous situations. However, he is mostly too stubborn or just shy in admitting his true feelings towards her.   \n\nIn the episode \"Perko's Secret Diary\", it was him who changed his mind last minute and decided to return Perko's diary to her without peeking into it's contents, showing his respect for her and her private life. Again in the episode \"Seeing Your True Identity! Perman is Mitsuo\", Mitsuo (as Perman) confesses to Perko directly that he knows that she likes her.  In the episode \"Goodbye Perko\", Mitsuo's copy robot stated that he knows Mitsuo's true feelings and urged Perman to stop Perko from resigning and not give into his stubbornness otherwise he may regret the decision for the rest of his life. In the same episode, he hurries to the location Perko was meant to get her memories as a Perman wiped and quit Perman and begs her to not do so even through the barrier Birdman put up and sheds tears when he fails to break through it. Later, when all the Permans combine the strength and break through it, he hugs Perko joyfully and later blushes at his own action along with Perko who did the same. This confirmed his feelings towards her. \n\nIn the last episode of the 1987 series, \"Perko's Treasure\", he seems to also resonate with her feelings as he takes the news of her liking him romantically, really well and is overjoyed by the fact that he is, indeed, very special to her and that she likes him.\n==== [[Michiko Sawada]] ====\n[[File:MichikowithPerman.jpeg|thumb|Michiko Holding Perman Hand]]\nMitsuo has a clear crush on Michiko and often tries to impress her, though he usually fails due to his clumsy nature. Michiko, who is popular and somewhat proud, enjoys being admired but does not see Mitsuo as more than a friend. However, she oftenshows admiration for Perman while not knowing that he is actually Mitsuo. This creates a typical childhood crush dynamic, where Mitsuo’s feelings are genuine but largely unnoticed, leading to many funny and awkward situations.\n\n== Etymology==\n\n* '''Mitsuo (ミツ夫) -''' Unusually, his name is written in a mix of katakana and kanji, instead of pure hiragana or kanji. In some older versions, his named was spelled \"満夫\", which can mean \"full man\".\n* '''Suwa (須羽) -''' This name also uses an unusual spelling, which can mean 'essential bird'.\n\nMitsuo's name contains wordplay that is only apparent in the original Japanese version. When written in kanji, it comes as '''須羽満夫''' (Suwa Mitsuo). Suwa sounds similar to \"Super\" in Japanese, resulting in \"Super Mitsuo\". Her hair is a bit curly.\n\n== Personality ==\nShe is a kind, gentle, polite and respectful girl and good at every household work. It is the girly nature of her that Mitsuo is so fond of, that's why most of the times preferring to have her company over Pako. But, Michiko is little bit self-centred herself as she always chooses Perman over Mitsuo, though both are the same person. Perman's power and helping nature fascinates her. Although there is nothing wrong to have a crush on Perman as everyone has their own preferences and fantasies, but she was so much crazy about being with Perman, that she failed to realize the same good qualities of Perman in Mitsuo, which often subjects  her to hatred. Perman fans also get irritated when Michiko tries to get in between Perman and Pako. But we also see her kind side in one episode named \"Michiko's Dream\", where she decided to give up her dream of flying with Perman seeing how much Perman and Pako understand each other and said, \"Both of you look so good together.
