He is a prodigy in inventing machines. He reads his encyclopedia and invents gadgets from the past. He can even repair normal machines and invent variations of the gadgets. Because of his mechanical attitude, he commands respect from his friends. He has a serious personality and is well-liked by his friends, but due to his serious personality he tells Korosuke and the others that he doesn't want to cheat to get good grades, but he has also made inventions just to skip physical education tests.\n\nHe was as first, sloppy and playful but as the story progresses, he becomes more calm and trusted by his friends, and in the later stages, he is often consulted about inventions. At first, his inventions were for pranks or fraudulent purposes, but after being persuaded by his father and reforming, he is depicted as being less likely to do so.\n\nHe also has a fickle side, becoming attracted to classmates and upperclassmen when they approach him.\n\nIn the future, he wants to study science at a university in the United States. In the future, he will become an inventor who will eliminate kitchen knives from Japanese homes. After graduating from high school, he went on to Takao University.\n\nHis catchphrase is \"That's impossible!\" In kindergarten, he was called \"Crybaby Ei-chan\".\n\n== Relationships ==\n\n=== Michiko Kite ===\nHis mother is strict whenever it comes to studying.
