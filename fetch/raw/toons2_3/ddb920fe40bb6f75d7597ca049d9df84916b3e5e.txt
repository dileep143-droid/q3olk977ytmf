Although Sumire is a girl, she is officially addressed as \"Perman 3\". However, her teammates call her \"Perko\". Her secret identity, which she never reveals even to her teammates (with exception of Mitsuo in final chapter), is a famous child star, who feels more free and comfortable as Pako. \n\n== Appearance ==\nSumire is a petite, charming young girl. Her hair is styled in a sleek, rounded bob that curves inward towards her jawline. She has a thick, straight-cut \"helmet bangs\" that cover her entire forehead ending just above her eyes. She wears a thin blue hairband or (alice band) placed just behind her bangs, which helps define the shape of the crown. Her hair features a subtle outward flick or \"ducktail\" at the very nape of the neck, contrasted by the smooth, rounded sides that tuck in behind or over her ears. As Sumire, she dresses like a celebrity. But as Pako, she wears her Perman set (a red helmet, green cape, and badge) and a pink frock. \n\n== Personality ==\nShe has a tomboyish, brave, bold, hot-headed, short-tempered and headstrong personality as Pako. As Pako, she acts bossy and gets angry very quickly or we can say she is always angry as default. She often argues with her fellow teammate Mitsuo and try to take some sort of revenge even for a very little argument and opinion of his she doesn't like, her actions can be considered as harmful (a little mistake and perman can get hurt), disrespect, ego driven. Pako is always dominating. As Sumire, she acts calm, polite, gentle and respectful; that's why many fans think she has dual personality. But it's not actually dual personality. \"Pako\" is most probably who Sumire is underneath a very rude and socially unacceptable person in a relationship. \"Superstar Hoshino Sumire\" can be a persona she has to put up for the sake of protecting her image as a celebrity as celebrities are bound to behave in a particular fashion. That's why she feels free when she is Pako. We've seen Sumire's bossy, short-tempered and hot-headed, rude nature numerous times without wearing Perman set talking to Mitsuo over communication badge. Yes, Perman set might give her unimaginable amount of physical strength, but \"Pako\" is who Sumire truely is. \n\n== Relationships ==\n=== [[Mitsuo Suwa]] ===\n\n''For more information on their relationship and the list of scenes that was centered around them, see [[Mitsuo Suwa and Sumire Hoshino|Relationship Between Mitsuo Suwa and Sumire Hoshino]]'' \n[[File:Screenshot 2026-03-26 231916.png|thumb|Perman Pako Hugging in Goodbye Pako]][[Mitsuo Suwa|Mitsuo]] is the most special person in Sumire's life.
