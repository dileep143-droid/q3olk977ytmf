His hands are two rubber balls; despite not being able to play rock-paper-scissors with them, some comic panels and animated scenes do depict him with mitten-like fingers. He is approximately 50 centimeters tall and 30 centimeters wide.\n\nKorosuke’s body colors have changed significantly between the publication of the original manga and the release of the 1987 TV special. In earlier color comic media, his head was pink or orange, and his body was red or yellow.\n\n[[File:Korosuke - 1987 TV special.png|thumb|Korosuke as he appears in the 1987 TV special.]]\nIn the [[90 min special|1987 special]], his head color was changed to light yellow, his body to yellow, and his nose to blue. Additionally, he now carried a samurai sword on his back, which had a yellow tip and a black sheath.\n\nFrom the anime series onward, his palette was changed yet again: his light yellow head color was kept, but his body is orange, his nose and two circles are now red, and his sword’s sheath is red.\n\n==History==\n\nKorosuke was the first invention built by Eiichi Kite.\n\nIn the final episode of the anime, it is revealed that Korosuke is modeled after Kiteretsu-Saisama’s deceased son.
