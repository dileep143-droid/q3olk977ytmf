'''Pogo''' is an Indian [[pay television channel]] owned and operated by [[Warner Bros. Discovery India]] under its International division, as part of [[Cartoon Network (India)|Cartoon Network India]] as the network's sister channel. It was launched on 1 January 2004.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |date=2003-11-25 |title=Turner International India to launch 24-hour kids' channel, POGO |url=https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/7709_turner-international-india-to-launch-24-hour-kids-channel-pogo |access-date= |website=afaqs! |archive-date=25 June 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210625172613/https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/7709_turner-international-india-to-launch-24-hour-kids-channel-pogo |url-status=live }}</ref> It broadcasts both animated and live action programming.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Staff |first=Variety |date=2003-12-07 |title=Turner kids’ Pogo bounces onscreen |url=https://variety.com/2003/tv/news/turner-kids-pogo-bounces-onscreen-1117896680/ |access-date=2026-09-27 |website=Variety |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Broadcast==
In India, Sri Lanka and the Maldives, Pogo is available as a 24-hour channel.<ref name=":1" /> A time-based block version is shown in [[Nepal]] and [[Bhutan]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://thebhutanese.bt/animation-program-ro-ngoedrup-chen-launched/|title=Animation program "Ro-Ngoedrup-Chen" launched|date=24 August 2013|access-date=25 June 2022|work=[[The Bhutanese]]|archive-date=27 May 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220527132841/https://thebhutanese.bt/animation-program-ro-ngoedrup-chen-launched/|url-status=live}}</ref>

Pogo is available as a block in [[Bangladesh]] and [[Pakistan]] on [[Cartoon Network (Pakistan)|Cartoon Network]].{{Citation needed|date=February 2024}}

In [[Thailand]] Pogo is available as a block on Family Channel 13.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://kidscreen.com/2015/03/30/turner-brings-pogo-to-thailand/ | title=Turner takes POGO to Thailand | access-date=30 March 2015 | archive-date=2 April 2015 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150402113320/http://kidscreen.com/2015/03/30/turner-brings-pogo-to-thailand/ | url-status=live }}</ref>

== History ==
===2004–2010===
[[File:POGO-logo.svg|thumb|left|The first logo, used from 2004 to 2016.]]
Pogo was officially launched on 1 January 2004 in [[English language|English]], [[Hindi]], [[Tamil language|Tamil]] by Turner International India.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Vaid |first=Sumita |date=2003-11-24 |title=Turner International India to launch 24-hour kids' channel, POGO |url=https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/7709_turner-international-india-to-launch-24-hour-kids-channel-pogo |access-date=2021-01-19 |website=afaqs! |archive-date=25 June 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210625172613/https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/7709_turner-international-india-to-launch-24-hour-kids-channel-pogo |url-status=live }}</ref> After its launch, the channel primarily broadcast programming from [[Cartoon Network]] and [[Boomerang (TV network)|Boomerang]] such as ''[[Tweenies]]'', ''[[Beakman's World]]'', ''[[Looney Tunes]]'', and ''[[Scooby-Doo]]''. {{cn|date=June 2026}}

The channel aired ''[[Wallace & Gromit]]'', ''[[Mr. Bean: The Animated Series]]'', ''[[Lois & Clark: The New Adventures of Superman]]'', and ''[[Walking with Dinosaurs]]''. during its starting days.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2003-11-25 |title=Turner International India to launch 24-hour kids' channel, POGO |url=https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/7709_turner-international-india-to-launch-24-hour-kids-channel-pogo |access-date=2023-10-03 |website=afaqs! |archive-date=25 June 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210625172613/https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/7709_turner-international-india-to-launch-24-hour-kids-channel-pogo |url-status=live }}</ref>

In 2004, the channel launched ''[[Takeshi's Castle]]'' and premiered the [[Harry Potter (film series)|''Harry Potter'' movies]].<ref name=":0" />

On January 2005, the channel celebrated its 2nd anniversary by featuring a special programming franchise '''Ek Se Bhale Do''' that included double episodes of the most popular shows and blockbuster movies shown on the channel at that time.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=POGO lines up special programming to mark second anniversary - Exchange4media |url=https://www.exchange4media.com/kids-news/pogo-lines-up-special-programming-to-mark-second-anniversary-19152.html |access-date=2023-10-03 |website=Indian Advertising Media & Marketing News – exchange4media |language=en}}</ref> On 2 January 2005, the channel launched a new initiative called ''POGO Funtakshari''.<ref name=":0" />

On 1 March 2005, Pogo had ''[[Takeshi's Castle]]'' dubbed over with commentary from Bollywood personality and anchor, [[Jaaved Jaaferi]].<ref>{{Cite web|date=24 February 2005|title=Pogo premiers Japanese game show 'Takeshi's Castle'|url=https://www.indiantelevision.com/headlines/y2k5/feb/feb254.htm|access-date=26 May 2022|website=Indian Television Dot Com|language=en|archive-date=26 May 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220526120837/https://www.indiantelevision.com/headlines/y2k5/feb/feb254.htm|url-status=live}}</ref>

On 6 June 2005, the channel premiered ''[[Shaktimaan]]''.<ref>{{Cite web |title='Shaktiman' to be aired on POGO |url=http://www.tellychakkar.com/tv/tv-news/shaktiman-be-aired-pogo |access-date=2023-10-03 |website=Tellychakkar.com |language=en}}</ref>The channel became 2nd most viewed channel in ratings after its sister channel [[Cartoon Network (Indian TV channel)|Cartoon Network]] which was on 1st.<ref name=":0" />

The channel launched its [[Tiny TV]] block featuring preschool shows.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2010-07-01 |title=Tiny TV makes comeback on POGO |url=https://www.adgully.com/tiny-tv-makes-a-comeback-on-pogo-43582.html |access-date=2023-10-03 |website=www.adgully.com |language=en-US |archive-date=4 October 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231004124211/https://www.adgully.com/tiny-tv-makes-a-comeback-on-pogo-43582.html |url-status=live }}</ref>

===2010–2013===
In 2010, Pogo began focusing more on live action programming than animated series.<ref>{{Cite web | date=2010-12-21 |title=Pogo M.A.D|url=https://www.adgully.com/pogo-launches-pogo-m-a-d-creations-art-curriculum-books-for-kg-to-viiith-std-45335.html |access-date=2023-10-03 |website=www.adgully.com |language=en-US}}</ref>

In June 2010, the channel premiered ''[[Kumbh Karan]]'',<ref name="deccanherald">{{Cite web |last=DHNS |title=Desi toons offer infotainment |url=https://www.deccanherald.com/features/desi-toons-offer-infotainment-2494875 |access-date=2023-10-03 |website=Deccan Herald |language=en}}</ref> and relaunched its preschool featuring block [[Tiny TV]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2010-07-01 |title=Tiny TV makes a comeback on POGO |url=https://www.adgully.com/tiny-tv-makes-a-comeback-on-pogo-43582.html |access-date=2023-10-03 |website=www.adgully.com |language=en-US |archive-date=4 October 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231004124211/https://www.adgully.com/tiny-tv-makes-a-comeback-on-pogo-43582.html |url-status=live }}</ref>

In 2011, Pogo began to compete with its sister channel [[Cartoon Network (India)|Cartoon Network]] in ratings which was leading in viewership at that time.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cartoon Network India - New Generations Research 2012 |url=https://www.slideshare.net/TheHWD/cartoon-network-new-generations-india-2012 |access-date=2023-10-03 |website=www.slideshare.net |language=en |archive-date=4 October 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231004124212/https://www.slideshare.net/TheHWD/cartoon-network-new-generations-india-2012 |url-status=live }}</ref> The channel began airing shows such as ''[[Batman]]'', ''[[The Powerpuff Girls]]'', and more. Pogo acquired the rights of ''[[Chhota Bheem]]'' and subsequently began airing the show, which became hugely successful in India.{{cn|date=June 2026}}

In 2012, Pogo became leading kids channel in India by surpassing its sister channel Cartoon Network in ratings and enjoyed to be the No. 1 kids channel in India. ''[[Chhota Bheem]]'' proven to be huge success for Pogo. The channel started airing more local shows including ''Chhota Bheem''.<ref name="deccanherald"/><ref>{{Cite web |last=Team |first=AnimationXpress |date=2012-05-23 |title=Double-trouble, twin superheroes - 'Kumbh Karan' are back on POGO Catch an exciting line-up of a new movie, new episodes and contest - |url=https://animationxpress.com/broadcast/double-trouble-twin-superheroes-kumbh-karan-are-back-on-pogo-catch-an-exciting-line-up-of-a-new-movie-new-episodes-and-contest/ |access-date=2023-10-03 |language=en-US}}</ref>

In February 2013, the [[Government of Bangladesh]] banned the broadcast of Pogo in the country, as it was accused of broadcasting there without authorisation.<ref>{{Cite news|title=Govt slaps ban on Hindi-dubbed Doraemon telecast|url=https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/2013/02/14/govt-slaps-ban-on-hindi-dubbed-doraemon-telecast|date=14 February 2013|access-date=21 June 2022|work=BDNews24|archive-date=26 May 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220526070302/https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/2013/02/14/govt-slaps-ban-on-hindi-dubbed-doraemon-telecast|url-status=live}}</ref>

On 6 June 2013, the channel premiered ''Krish, Trish and Baltiboy''.<ref>{{Cite web |title=POGO set to premiere all new Desi Shows |url=https://www.business-standard.com/article/press-releases/pogo-set-to-premiere-all-new-desi-toon-110060300110_1.html |access-date=6 June 2013 |website=Business Standard |archive-date=24 November 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131124064356/http://www.business-standard.com/article/press-releases/pogo-set-to-premiere-all-new-desi-toon-110060300110_1.html |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Busting the myth of Indian cartoons |url=https://www.dnaindia.com/lifestyle/report-busting-the-myth-of-indian-cartoons-1530163 |access-date=2023-10-03 |website=DNA India |language=en |archive-date=4 October 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231004124211/https://www.dnaindia.com/lifestyle/report-busting-the-myth-of-indian-cartoons-1530163 |url-status=live }}</ref>

===2014–2017===
[[File:POGO-logo.png|thumb|left|A variation of Pogo's current logo, used as of 7 November 2016.]]
In 2014, the channel celebrated its 10th anniversary.<ref>{{Cite web |title=POGO Amazing Kids Awards 2013 celebrates a decade of recognising the most talented kids across India |url=http://www.tellychakkar.com/tv/releases/pogo-amazing-kids-awards-2013-celebrates-decade-of-recognising-the-most-talented-kids |access-date=2023-08-18 |website=Tellychakkar.com |language=en |archive-date=18 August 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230818123427/https://www.tellychakkar.com/tv/releases/pogo-amazing-kids-awards-2013-celebrates-decade-of-recognising-the-most-talented-kids |url-status=live }}</ref>In January 2014, the channel premiered ''Obbochama-Kun'' and premiered ''Chhota Bheem - Neeli Pahaadi Movie'' on 26 January.<ref>{{Cite web |title=New shows, movies and exciting contests all month long on Cartoon Network & POGO |url=http://www.tellychakkar.com/tv/releases/new-shows-movies-and-exciting-contests-all-month-long-cartoon-network-pogo-526 |access-date=2023-10-03 |website=Tellychakkar.com |language=en |archive-date=4 October 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231004124316/https://www.tellychakkar.com/tv/releases/new-shows-movies-and-exciting-contests-all-month-long-cartoon-network-pogo-526 |url-status=live }}</ref>

In 2015, the channel was launched in [[Thailand]] as a block on ''Family Channel 13''.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Turner takes POGO to Thailand |url=https://kidscreen.com/2015/03/30/turner-brings-pogo-to-thailand/ |access-date=2023-10-03 |archive-date=30 June 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220630124528/https://kidscreen.com/2015/03/30/turner-brings-pogo-to-thailand/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

On 19 December 2015, the channel premiered ''[[New Looney Tunes|Wabbit]]''.<ref>{{Cite web |title=POGO launches new series 'Wabbit' - A Looney Toons Production |url=http://www.tellychakkar.com/tv/releases/pogo-launches-new-series-wabbit-looney-toons-production-151217 |access-date=2023-10-04 |website=Tellychakkar.com |language=en |archive-date=9 October 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231009090354/https://www.tellychakkar.com/tv/releases/pogo-launches-new-series-wabbit-looney-toons-production-151217 |url-status=live }}</ref>

In January 2016, Pogo premiered ''[[Yo-kai Watch|Yokai Watch]]'' on 11 January and Tashi on 23 January.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |date=2016-01-12 |title=Pogo lines up two new international shows in January |url=https://www.indiantelevision.com/television/tv-channels/kids/pogo-lines-up-two-new-international-shows-in-january-160112 |access-date=2022-05-26 |website=Indian Television Dot Com |language=en |archive-date=26 May 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220526123850/https://www.indiantelevision.com/television/tv-channels/kids/pogo-lines-up-two-new-international-shows-in-january-160112 |url-status=live }}</ref>

In 2017, Pogo rebranded with a new logo and look and the channel began classic shows from [[Hanna-Barbera]], such as ''[[Tom and Jerry]]'' and ''[[Scooby-Doo]]''. ''[[Grizzy & the Lemmings]]'' started to air on the channel on 2 January, ''[[Oddbods]]'' on 9 January and ''[[Andy Pirki]]'' on 3 December.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Pogo Channel Rebrand / Art&Graft |url=https://artandgraft.com/project/pogo-rebrand |access-date=2023-10-04 |website=Art&Graft |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last= |date=2017-01-03 |title=Pogo welcomes New Year with new shows, movie mania |url=https://www.indiantelevision.com/television/tv-channels/kids/pogo-welcomes-new-year-with-new-shows-movie-mania-170103 |access-date=2022-05-26 |website=Indian Television Dot Com |language=en |archive-date=26 May 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220526122344/https://www.indiantelevision.com/television/tv-channels/kids/pogo-welcomes-new-year-with-new-shows-movie-mania-170103 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last= |date=2017-11-25 |title=Pogo launches indigenous slapstick comedy Andy Pirki |url=https://indiantelevision.com/television/tv-shows/animation/pogo-launches-indigenous-slapstick-comedy-andy-pirki-171125 |access-date=2022-05-26 |website=Indian Television Dot Com |language=en |archive-date=14 August 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190814005720/http://www.indiantelevision.com/television/tv-shows/animation/pogo-launches-indigenous-slapstick-comedy-andy-pirki-171125 |url-status=live }}</ref>

===2018–2021===
From 2018 to 2020, the channel started to shift its focus more on broadcasting mostly [[Indian animation industry|Indian animated series]] ''Chhota Bheem'', ''Mighty Raju'', ''[[Tik Tak Tail]]'', etc. and hence stopped some of the international shows.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Pogo & Cartoon Network go local with India Originals - Exchange4media|url=https://www.exchange4media.com/media-tv-news/pogo-cartoon-network-go-local-with-india-originals-103113.html|access-date=2021-01-19|website=Indian Advertising Media & Marketing News – exchange4media|language=en|archive-date=5 February 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220205083157/https://www.exchange4media.com/media-tv-news/pogo-cartoon-network-go-local-with-india-originals-103113.html|url-status=live}}</ref> In 2021, Pogo stopped all international shows altogether and only aired Indian animation, like ''Chhota Bheem'', ''Dabangg'', ''[[Roll No 21]]'', ''Lambuji Tinguji'' and ''[[Bandbudh Aur Budbak]]''.

Pogo TV became the third most watched kids channel across all genres with [[Audience measurement|TRP]] in December 2020.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Barc trp data|title=Barc report. DATA Week 39: Saturday, 26th September 2020 To Friday, 2nd October 2020|url=https://barcindia.co.in/data-insights|access-date=5 October 2020|website=barcindia.co.in|archive-date=11 November 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201111165652/https://barcindia.co.in/data-insights|url-status=live}}</ref>

Pogo aired an animated movie ''Shaktimaan New TV Special'' on 14 November 2021.<ref>{{Citation|title=Shaktimaan {{!}} New TV Special {{!}} Sunday 14th November, 10 PM {{!}} Pogo TV|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SrMzQbD2Q5A|language=en|access-date=2021-11-09|archive-date=2 September 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220902110247/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SrMzQbD2Q5A|url-status=live}}</ref> A [[Telugu language|Telugu-language]] audio track was added again to Pogo replacing the [[English language|English-language]] audio track on 30 November 2021.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.animationxpress.com/animation/kids-favourite-pogo-is-now-available-in-telugu/|title=Kids' favourite POGO is now available in Telugu|date=30 November 2021|access-date=12 July 2022|work=Animation Xpress|archive-date=12 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220712064826/https://www.animationxpress.com/animation/kids-favourite-pogo-is-now-available-in-telugu/|url-status=live}}</ref>
