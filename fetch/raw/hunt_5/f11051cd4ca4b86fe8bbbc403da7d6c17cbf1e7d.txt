His skin color is blue in this series.\nKanishk: Kanishk is the incarnation of Kansa who is the principal of Mathura Anath Ashram. Kanishk is very concerned about the performance of his school and its result. Besides being a dentist, J is also a scientist who keeps inventing various machines, devices, and traps mostly used to tackle Kris. However, he often desires to be the principal of the school and hopes to kick Kanishk out because Kanishk keeps whacking and humiliating him. He gets help from Kris at times.\nPinky: Pinky is Kris' classmate and a student of Mathura Anath Ashram. She is the incarnation of Radha.
