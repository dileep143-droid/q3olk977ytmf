The series began as a student film called ''Whoopass Stew'', made by McCracken while he attended the [[California Institute of the Arts]] in 1992. Two additional shorts, \"Meat Fuzzy Lumpkins\" and \"Crime 101\", later aired on Cartoon Network's ''[[What a Cartoon!|World Premiere Toons]]'' in 1995 and 1996. The series officially premiered on November 18, 1998, lasting 6 seasons with 78 episodes total. A [[The Powerpuff Girls Movie|feature film]], a Christmas special, and a 10th anniversary special were produced for the series.\n\nEpisodes of ''The Powerpuff Girls'' have seen numerous [[VHS]] and [[DVD]] releases as well. The musical episode \"[[#ep61|See Me, Feel Me, Gnomey]]\" was never aired in the United States, but was broadcast on [[YTV (Canadian TV channel)|YTV]] in Canada in 2004\u20132005 as well as in other
