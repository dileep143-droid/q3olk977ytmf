[[Tomohisa Taguchi]] was appointed director and series composer, with Masashi Kudo returning as character designer, [[Shirō Sagisu]] returning to compose the music, and [[Studio Pierrot]] producing the animation.<ref>{{#invoke:Cite|web|last=Harding|first=Daryl|title=Bleach Thousand-Year Blood War TV Anime Trailer, Key Visual Released for October 2022 Start|url=https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/latest/2021/12/18/first-bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-arc-tv-anime-trailer-key-visual-released-for-october-2022-start|website=[[Crunchyroll]]|date=December 18, 2021|access-date=August 23, 2026|archive-date=August 24, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260824030545/https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/latest/2021/12/18/first-bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-arc-tv-anime-trailer-key-visual-released-for-october-2022-start|url-status=live}}</ref> Kubo is credited on the series as the creator of the original work and general supervisor.<ref>{{#invoke:Cite|web|url=https://bleach-anime.com/en/staffcast/|title=Staff & Cast|website=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War Official Website|access-date=August 23, 2026|archive-date=August 23, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260823141021/https://bleach-anime.com/en/staffcast/|url-status=live}}</ref>\n\nSeries composition writer Masaki Hiramatsu described adapting the manga as a form of \"translation\", noting that the pacing of a weekly manga differs from that of a television episode. Major changes and material not depicted in the manga were discussed with [[Tite Kubo]], who supervised revised and original scenes and could request changes of his own.<ref name=\"HiramatsuInterview\">{{Cite web|script-title=ja:シリーズ構成 平松正樹インタビュー|trans-title=Interview with Series Composition Writer Masaki Hiramatsu|url=https://bleach-anime.com/special/interview/06.html|website=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War Official Website|language=ja|access-date=August 23, 2026|archive-date=August 23, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260823234454/https://bleach-anime.com/special/interview/06.html|url-status=live}}</ref> Hiramatsu cited the sixth episode's meeting between [[Sōsuke Aizen]] and [[Yhwach]] as an example, with Kubo substantially rewriting the dialogue for the expanded anime scene.<ref name=\"HiramatsuInterview\"/>\n\nThe amount of anime-original material increased as production continued. For the second [[Television in Japan#Programs|cours]], the staff stated that original scenes were first proposed by the animation team and then submitted to Kubo, who sometimes returned rough manga-style storyboards to clarify his ideas. The additions ranged from extra dialogue and explanatory material to scenes not depicted in the manga.<ref name=\"Part2Production\">{{Cite web|script-title=ja:シリーズ構成・平松正樹様×チーフ演出・村田光様×プロデューサー・富永禎彦様（studioぴえろ）インタビュー|trans-title=Interview with Masaki Hiramatsu, Hikaru Murata and Yoshihiko Tominaga|url=https://bleach-anime.com/special/interview02/18.html|website=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War Official Website|language=ja|access-date=August 23, 2026|archive-date=August 23, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260823144923/https://bleach-anime.com/special/interview02/18.html|url-status=live}}</ref> Kubo said that, for an original battle in the second cours that he had been unable to depict in the manga, he supplied the production staff with approximately five or six pages of illustrations showing the characters' movements, transitions and fighting sequence.<ref>{{#invoke:Cite|web|title=Tite Kubo x Masakazu Morita Interview|url=https://www.viz.com/blog/posts/tite-kubo-x-masakazu-morita-interview|website=[[Viz Media]]|date=April 10, 2023|access-date=August 23, 2026|archive-date=August 23, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260823153336/https://www.viz.com/blog/posts/tite-kubo-x-masakazu-morita-interview|url-status=live}}</ref> By production of the second cours, Kubo was also attending most recording sessions in person and provided direction or clarification to the voice cast when necessary.<ref name=\"Part2Production\"/>\n\nFor the third cours, ''The Conflict'', Taguchi became chief series director while Hikaru Murata became series director, with animation production credited to Pierrot Films. Taguchi supervised the overall scripts and storyboards, while Murata oversaw individual episode direction and production meetings.<ref>{{#invoke:Cite|web|script-title=ja:総監督・田口智久×監督・村田光インタビュー|trans-title=Interview with Chief Series Director Tomohisa Taguchi and Series Director Hikaru Murata|url=https://bleach-anime.com/special/interview03/29.html|website=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War Official Website|language=ja|access-date=August 23, 2026|archive-date=August 23, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260823212155/https://bleach-anime.com/special/interview03/29.html|url-status=live}}</ref> Taguchi stated that the staff's working relationship with Kubo had become closer over the first two cours, making it easier to consult him about newly created scenes. Murata said that ''The Conflict'' contained more anime-original material than the preceding cours, with the additional material produced under Kubo's supervision and designed to connect back into the manga's storyline.<ref>{{#invoke:Cite|web|script-title=ja:『BLEACH』原作にないシーン増加へ 某作品に匹敵するクオリティ目指し制作「費用は嵩むしスケジュールは長期（笑）」|trans-title=Bleach to Increase Scenes Not in Original Manga; Production Aims for High Quality|url=https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2352374/full/|website=[[Oricon|Oricon News]]|language=ja|date=November 1, 2024|access-date=August 23, 2026|archive-date=August 23, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260823200621/https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2352374/full/|url-status=live}}</ref> Taguchi and Murata retained their respective positions for the fourth and final cours, ''The Calamity'', with Pierrot Films continuing as the animation studio.<ref>{{#invoke:Cite|web|url=https://bleach-anime.com/en/staffcast/|title=Staff & Cast|website=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War Official Website|access-date=August 23, 2026|archive-date=August 23, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260823141021/https://bleach-anime.com/en/staffcast/|url-status=live}}</ref>\n\n== Music ==\n{{See also|List of Bleach soundtracks{{!}}List of ''Bleach'' soundtracks}}\n\nThe music for ''Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War'' was composed by [[Shirō Sagisu]], who also composed the score for the original ''[[Bleach (TV series)|Bleach]]'' anime. ==\n=== Critical response ===\n''Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War'' received generally positive reviews from critics, with particular praise directed toward its animation, action sequences, visual presentation, music, and adaptation of the manga's final storyline. Just Lunning of ''[[IGN]]'' gave ''The Blood Warfare'' an 8 out of 10, praising the series's visual presentation and describing its action scenes as \"crisp and inventive\".<ref name=\"IGNBloodWarfare\">{{cite web|last=Lunning|first=Just|title=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War Review|work=[[IGN]]|date=October 18, 2022|url=https://www.ign.com/articles/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-review|access-date=September 16, 2026}}</ref> Brittany Vincent of ''[[Decider (website)|Decider]]'' described the revival as a welcoming return for longtime viewers, while Panos Kotzathanasis of ''Asian Movie Pulse'' praised the development of the series's artwork and considered the adaptation a strong continuation of ''Bleach''.<ref name=\"RTBloodWarfare\">{{cite web|title=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War: The Blood Warfare|work=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/bleach_thousand_year_blood_war/s01|access-date=September 16, 2026}}</ref>\n\nThe series's production values were frequently highlighted by critics. Lunning described ''Thousand-Year Blood War'' as an \"impressive transformation\" of the series and praised its action choreography.<ref name=\"IGNBloodWarfare\" /> Adrian Ruiz of ''But Why Tho?'' similarly praised the animation produced by [[Studio Pierrot]], the soundtrack, and the action in his review of the premiere.<ref>{{cite web|last=Ruiz|first=Adrian|title=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War Episode 1 Review: The Blood Warfare|work=But Why Tho?|date=April 15, 2023|url=https://butwhytho.net/2023/04/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-episode-1-review-the-blood-warfare/|access-date=September 16, 2026}}</ref>\n\nReception to the second cours, ''The Separation'', was similarly positive. Kotzathanasis gave the cours a 7 out of 10, writing that, despite some clichés and other excesses, it remained among the stronger shōnen anime productions of its period.<ref name=\"RTSeparation\">{{cite web|title=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War: The Separation|work=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/bleach_thousand_year_blood_war/s02|access-date=September 16, 2026}}</ref> Vincent of ''Decider'' praised the cours for returning viewers to the action and setting up the concluding portion of the storyline.<ref name=\"RTSeparation\" /> ''Rotten Tomatoes'' lists ''The Separation'' with a 94% Tomatometer score based on two critic reviews, although the small number of reviews limits the extent to which the aggregate score can be considered representative of broader critical opinion.<ref name=\"RTSeparation\" />\n\nThe pacing and structure of the adaptation received more mixed commentary. Ruiz, reviewing an episode centered on Kenpachi Zaraki and Retsu Unohana, noted that the series had slowed considerably after its initial action-heavy episodes, but praised the character development and the episode's focus on the two characters.<ref>{{cite web|last=Ruiz|first=Adrian|title=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War Episode 10 Review: The Battle|work=But Why Tho?|date=December 17, 2022|url=https://butwhytho.net/2022/12/review-bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-episode-10-the-battle/|access-date=September 16, 2026}}</ref>\n\nThe third cours, ''The Conflict'', continued to receive positive critical attention. Panos Kotzathanasis of ''Asian Movie Pulse'' reviewed the cours favorably, and ''Rotten Tomatoes'' lists it with a 99% Tomatometer score based on a single critic review.<ref>{{cite web|title=Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War: The Conflict|work=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/bleach_thousand_year_blood_war/s03|access-date=September 16, 2026}}</ref> Because the aggregate is based on only one critic review, it does not by itself establish a broad critical consensus.\n\n=== Accolades ===\nThe anime's ''Thousand-Year Blood War – The Separation'' was nominated for
