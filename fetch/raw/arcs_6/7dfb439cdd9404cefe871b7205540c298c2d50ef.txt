animanga/Footer|portal=yes}}\n\n{{Nihongo|'''''Black Clover'''''|ブラッククローバー|Burakku Kurōbā|lead=yes}} is a Japanese [[manga]] series written and illustrated by [[Yūki Tabata]]. It started in [[Shueisha]]'s [[Shōnen manga|{{Transliteration|ja|shōnen}} manga]] magazine ''[[Weekly Shōnen Jump]]'' in February 2015. The series ran in the magazine until August 2023, after which it moved to ''[[Jump Giga]]'' in December of that same year and finished in May 2026. Its chapters were collected in 38 {{Transliteration|ja|[[tankōbon]]}} volumes. Set in a world where people are born with the ability to use magic, the story follows [[Asta (Black Clover)|Asta]], a young boy without any magic power who is given a rare [[grimoire]] that grants him anti-magic abilities. With his fellow mages from the Black Bulls, Asta plans to become the next Wizard King.\n\nThe manga was first adapted into an [[original video animation]] (OVA) by [[Xebec (studio)|Xebec Zwei]], released in 2017. A 170-episode [[anime]] television series adaptation produced by [[Pierrot (company)|Pierrot]] aired on [[TV Tokyo]] from October 2017 to March 2021; a second season is set to premiere in October 2026. An anime film, titled ''[[Black Clover: Sword of the Wizard King]]'', premiered simultaneously in Japanese theaters and internationally on [[Netflix]] in June 2023.\n\nBy September 2026, the manga had [[List of best-selling manga|over 25 million copies in circulation]] worldwide.\n\n== Plot ==\n{{Main|List of Black Clover characters{{!}}List of ''Black Clover'' characters}}\n\nThe series focuses on [[Asta (Black Clover)|Asta]], a young orphan who is left to be raised in an orphanage in a rural village alongside his fellow orphan, Yuno. While everyone is born with the ability to utilize ''mana'' in the form of magical power, Asta was born without mana, and thus has no way to use magic. Instead, he focuses on physical strength. Conversely, Yuno was born as a prodigy with immense magical power and the talent to control wind magic.\n\nMotivated by a desire to become the next Wizard King, an authority figure second to the king of Clover Kingdom, the two youths developed a friendly rivalry. Yuno obtains a legendary four-leaf grimoire held by the kingdom's first Wizard King. The four-leaf grimoire is a rare grimoire, only given to the most immense mages. Asta, despite his lack of magic, obtained an enigmatic five-leaf grimoire that contains mysterious elf swords and a bodiless member of the Devil race who utilizes rare anti-magic. Afterwards, Asta and Yuno each join a Magic Knight squad as the first step to fulfill their ambitions.\n\nAsta joins the Black Bulls under [[Yami Sukehiro]] alongside [[Noelle Silva]], while Yuno becomes a member of the Golden Dawn. They embark on various adventures while contending with an extremist group called the Eye of the Midnight Sun, whose leadership is manipulated by a Devil in avenging an injustice committed against the Elves by the Clover Kingdom at the time of its founding. The Magic Knights then face the Dark Triad of the Spade Kingdom, with Asta and Yuno learning of their Devils' influence on their lives and of the Dark Triad's plan to fully manifest the Devils into their world. Then the Magic Knights face the Dark Triad's eldest sibling, Lucius Zogratis, who wants to create his own perfect world.\n\n== Production ==\nManga author [[Yūki Tabata]] started in the manga industry at the age of 20 and worked as an assistant for seven years before his [[science fiction]] [[one-shot (comics)|one-shot]] ''Hungry Joker'' was briefly serialized in the [[Shueisha]] magazine ''[[Weekly Shōnen Jump]]'' for 24 chapters from November 12, 2012, to May 13, 2013, before being canceled.<ref>{{cite web|last=Hodgkins|first=Crystalyn|title=Shonen Jump to Launch 3 New Manga Series in November|url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2012-10-31/shonen-jump-to-launch-3-new-manga-series-in-november|website=[[Anime News Network]]|date=October 21, 2012|access-date=June 10, 2016|archive-date=May 31, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190531025706/https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2012-10-31/shonen-jump-to-launch-3-new-manga-series-in-november|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|date=May 13, 2013|script-title=ja:｢LIGHT WING｣の神海英雄、ジャンプで吹奏楽マンガ連載|url=https://natalie.mu/comic/news/90483|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191230053331/https://natalie.mu/comic/news/90483|archive-date=December 30, 2019|access-date=February 2, 2021|website=[[Comic Natalie]]|publisher=Natasha, Inc|language=ja}}</ref><ref name=\"ComicsAlliance\">{{cite web|last=Speelman|first=Tom|url=https://comicsalliance.com/black-clover-readers-guide/|title=Big Spells, Bigger Swords: Should You Be Reading 'Black Clover'?|website=[[ComicsAlliance]]|date=March 17, 2016|access-date=June 10, 2016|archive-date=June 16, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160616124607/https://comicsalliance.com/black-clover-readers-guide/|url-status=live}}</ref> Tabata considered this manga a failure which he attributed largely to its quiet and dark-natured main character, who was very unlike the author himself. For this latter aspect, he took ideas from the {{Transliteration|ja|shōnen}} fighting manga ''[[Dragon Ball (manga)|Dragon Ball]]'' by [[Akira Toriyama]].<ref name=\"lepoint\"/> Tabata recounted he was inspired to become a manga artist after seeing the ''[[Dragon Ball Z]]'' [[anime]] series as a child then learning it was an adaptation of Toriyama's work.<ref name=\"francetv\"/><ref>{{cite book|author=[[Yūki Tabata|Tabata Yūki]]|date=August 7, 2018|title=Black Clover|volume=12|isbn=978-1-9747-0040-0|publisher=[[Viz Media]]|chapter=The Assorted Questions Brigade}}</ref> He stated that he was also a fan of other {{Transliteration|ja|shōnen}} series, such as ''[[YuYu Hakusho]]'' by [[Yoshihiro Togashi]] and ''[[Bleach (manga)|Bleach]]'' by [[Tite Kubo]].<ref>{{cite book|author=[[Yūki Tabata|Tabata Yūki]]|date=September 3, 2019|title=Black Clover|volume=17|isbn=978-1-9747-0616-7|publisher=[[Viz Media]]|chapter=The Assorted Questions Brigade}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=Whalen|first=Amanda|title=''Shōnen Jump'' Authors Comment on ''Bleach'' Ending|url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/interest/2016-08-22/shonen-jump-authors-comment-on-bleach-ending/.105535|website=[[Anime News Network]]|date=August 22, 2016|access-date=March 7, 2022|archive-date=November 9, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211109114002/https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/interest/2016-08-22/shonen-jump-authors-comment-on-bleach-ending/.105535|url-status=live}}</ref> Tabata wished to create a diverse cast of [[archetype|archetypical]] characters with opposing attributes. ===\nWhen reviewing the series for ''[[Townsquare Media|ComicsAlliance]]'', Tom Speelman described the premise as \"what if Harry Potter was a knight and also kinda dumb?\" He recommended it for fans of ''[[Naruto]]'' and ''[[Fairy Tail]]'', noting its similarities to the former series as well as to ''[[Bleach (manga)|Bleach]]''. He praised the author's ability to invigorate stock characters.<ref name=\"ComicsAlliance\"/> Henry Ma of ''[[Ka Leo O Hawaii]]'' praised the series' humor and art, noting that the latter was \"very nice\" and was similar to ''Fairy Tail''.<ref>{{cite web|last=Ma|first=Henry|title=Manga Mondays: 'Black Clover' special review|url=https://www.manoanow.org/kaleo/features/manga-mondays-black-clover-special-review/article_91e95000-e784-11e4-b2b9-ff9c6360389b.html|website=[[Ka Leo O Hawaii]]|date=April 20,
